## Come hack on chestnuts\![Permalink](https://blog.comma.ai/comma-hack-7/#come-hack-on-chestnuts "Permalink")

What does your embedded project look like with 172 TFLOPs? **[Chestnut](https://blog.comma.ai/chestnut/) is the easiest way to throw real GPU compute onto your embedded projects.**

Chestnut connects to any device with a USB port, all the way down to USB2. That means it works with your laptop, Raspberry Pi, comma four, and even your Android phone.

![chestnut connected to a desktop GPU and comma four](https://blog.comma.ai/img/chestnut/cover.jpg)

## I want to attend [Permalink](https://blog.comma.ai/comma-hack-7/#i-want-to-attend "Permalink")

We’re handpicking the strongest applicants on a rolling basis until the event. Great GitHub profiles, [leaderboard submissions](https://comma.ai/leaderboard), and [openpilot contributions](https://github.com/commaai/openpilot) are a plus.

Want us to fly you out? We like winners, so we’ll cover flights for the first two people who get a top 3 score on any active [leaderboard challenge](https://comma.ai/leaderboard).

## FAQ [Permalink](https://blog.comma.ai/comma-hack-7/#faq "Permalink")

**Do I need a chestnut?** Nope. We’ll provide the comma hardware and GPUs.

**What kind of projects will I work on?** That’s up to you. Build with chestnut, work on openpilot, or make something entirely new.

**How are projects judged?** Demos on Sunday afternoon, judged by the comma team on quality and creativity.
