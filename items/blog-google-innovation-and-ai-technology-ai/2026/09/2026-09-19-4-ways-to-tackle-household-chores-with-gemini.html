Imagine a typical Sunday evening: You’re sorting through bills, drafting a grocery list, and trying to figure out why your dishwasher is making a weird noise. This unpaid, often invisible mental labor and logistical grind is required to keep a household running.

While much of the conversation around AI focuses on how people are using it in the workplace, Google’s AI & Economy ATLAS v1.0 study reveals how it’s helping in other ways, too. After analyzing 15 million de-identified interactions, researchers found that over 86% of conversational AI interactions occur entirely outside of formal work.

Instead of just focusing on corporate tasks, many people are using Gemini to conquer their household to-do lists. Here are four ways you can use Gemini to reclaim your time:

1. Troubleshoot DIY repairs and home maintenance.

Get step-by-step guidance to set up toys, care for your car, or repair household appliances. Next time your internet is down, try tapping the camera icon in Gemini Live and asking, "What does this blinking light on the modem mean?"

2. Streamline your meal planning.

Build customized meal plans and matching grocery lists tailored to dietary restrictions or picky eaters. You can also pan your camera across your fridge shelves using Gemini Live and ask, “What can I make with these leftovers?” Gemini will identify ingredients and suggest recipes on the spot, which could help you reduce food waste. Connecting Instacart lets you instantly add ingredients to your cart and check out in a few taps, too.

3. Track your household spending.

Keep your finances organized and stick to your budget without the tedious manual data entry. Simply upload photos of your receipts for the month and let Gemini extract the information. For example, after a family vacation, upload your receipt photos and ask Gemini, "Summarize my Mexico expenses in USD."

4. Navigate big household purchases with ease.

Compare complex purchases — like family cars or appliances — without spending hours scrolling. Try asking Gemini to compare top-rated hybrid SUVs for a family of four, and you’ll get a side-by-side comparison table. At the dealership, you can also ask Gemini for tips to negotiate a better deal.

These productivity gains rarely show up in traditional economic data, but their real-world impact could be massive. Researchers estimate that saving households an average of just 30 minutes a week with everyday AI could add up to roughly $100 billion in annual economic value for the U.S. alone. By helping with day-to-day life, Gemini is giving people back their most valuable resource: time.
