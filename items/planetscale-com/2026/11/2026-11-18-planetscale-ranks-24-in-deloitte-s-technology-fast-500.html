PlanetScale is #24 on the 2026 Deloitte Technology Fast 500, a ranking of the fastest-growing technology companies in North America. Now in its 32nd year, the list ranks companies by revenue growth over a three-year period. To qualify, companies submit their revenue figures along with financial documentation that Deloitte reviews to verify the growth rate.

This year's ranking measures growth from fiscal year 2022 to fiscal year 2025, during which PlanetScale grew revenue by 3,493%. We also ranked #1 fastest-growing tech companies in the cloud services category.

This is our second time on the list, having ranked 188th in 2023. Back then, PlanetScale was a MySQL platform built on Vitess, the technology behind some of the largest MySQL deployments in the world. Three years later, we've added Postgres and Neki, sharded Postgres, to the platform. We continue to power the databases behind some of the fastest-growing companies in tech.

Here's a look at some of our major releases since our last ranking:

  • Metal. In March 2025, we launched PlanetScale Metal, which runs your database on locally attached NVMe drives instead of network-attached storage. That means unlimited I/O on every M- cluster size, and much faster, more predictable performance while saving money.
  • Postgres. In September 2025, PlanetScale for Postgres became generally available, bringing the performance of Metal and the operational maturity of PlanetScale to Postgres. In November, we made our Postgres databases even more accessible for startups and solo developers with databases starting at $5 per month.
  • Neki. Neki brings Vitess scale to Postgres. Built by the team behind Vitess, it gives you horizontal sharding, online resharding, non-blocking schema changes, and zero-downtime upgrades. Neki is already running production workloads.
  • Database Traffic Control™. In March 2026, we launched Traffic Control, which lets you set resource budgets for your Postgres query traffic so a runaway workload can't take down your database.
  • More places to run. Postgres and Neki are now available on Microsoft Azure, alongside AWS and GCP.

We've also kept investing in Vitess, which powers customers like Cursor, Cash App, Fin, and Square.
