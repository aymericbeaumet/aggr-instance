Did you realize the film was actually manipulating \*you\*?

*“You Can See Everything” is the new documentary about Elizabeth Holmes and Theranos. This is a no-spoilers review from a longtime profile writer. The film shapes how you feel about her through framing, editing and the timing of its reveals. Here’s how it works, and what to watch for.*

*\*\*\**

I saw the new Elizabeth Holmes documentary, *You Can See Everything*, last night, and I genuinely can’t type fast enough to express how mind-bending it was.

I’ve seen a lot of chatter online about whether Elizabeth Holmes is a liar, whether she deserves to be in prison for her failed startup Theranos, and whether the documentary is too sympathetic to her.

But that’s not even remotely close to what I took away from it. As we walked out, my husband asked me, “So what did you think?” And I just responded with, “Forget Elizabeth Holmes. That was a masterpiece in storytelling.”

I walked in expecting that the filmmakers were going to tell us a story about deception. What I did not expect is that they were deceiving us — the viewers — in real time…
