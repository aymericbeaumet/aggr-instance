like Claude, LM Studio Bionic, Gemini, and the suite from Topaz Labs — so you can learn faster, think in new ways, and get more done.

Affinity, Mail

AI features in apps

### Use intelligent tools to unlock new levels of productivity and creativity —

from searching faster in Mail and generating images in Affinity to editing in Adobe Photoshop and Final Cut Pro.

### Great powers come with  great privacy.

**Apple Intelligence and Siri AI are designed to protect your privacy at every step.** With powerful on-device processing, Apple Intelligence is aware of your personal information without collecting your personal information. And with groundbreaking Private Cloud Compute, Apple Intelligence can draw on larger server-based models, without making your data accessible to Apple or anyone else.

iMac is also available with a **nano‑texture glass option.** This reduces reflections and glare to eliminate distractions while maintaining outstanding image quality. It’s perfect for spaces with challenging lighting conditions — whether you’re near a window or under bright indoor lights.

Center Stage

### Center Stage.

The 12MP Center Stage camera puts the focus on you — and keeps it there. Stay perfectly framed during video calls, even as you move around or when more people join in.

Desk View

### Desk View.

Share a top-down view of your workspace — ideal for giving online music lessons or showing off your latest project.

**Studio-quality mics.** **The microphones on iMac make sure your voice comes through crisp and clear. And with directional beamforming, everyone hears you — not what’s going on around you.**

**Six-speaker sound system.** **iMac brings incredible audio to any space. Two pairs of force-cancelling woofers with two tweeters fill the room with bold, bass-rich sound. And Spatial Audio with support for Dolby Atmos creates an immersive listening experience.**

iPhone Mirroring

### iPhone Mirroring.

See and use what’s on your iPhone from your iMac, without having to reach for it.[6](https://www.apple.com/imac/#footnote-10) Move files, photos, and videos between devices as easily as you drag and drop between apps on Mac.

Universal Clipboard

### Universal Clipboard.

Lets you copy text, images, photos, and videos on your iPhone and paste to your iMac — or vice versa.

Calls and texts

### Calls and texts.

Calls and texts from your iPhone come right to your iMac, so you don’t need to switch devices when you’re in the zone.[7](https://www.apple.com/imac/#footnote-11)
