Siri AI, powered by Apple Intelligence, comes built into Apple HomeView to help you find what you need and get more done while protecting your privacy.[1](https://www.apple.com/apple-homeview/#footnote-2) Siri AI adapts to each person and surfaces what matters most to them — all they have to do is ask.

Available in English to start.\
Usage limits may apply.

Personal Context

### Relevant to you.

Pull up a party invitation from your calendar, find RSVPs buried in your messages, or play the perfect soundtrack to match the vibe. Siri AI works with your paired iPhone or iPad to find relevant content just for you.[1](https://www.apple.com/apple-homeview/#footnote-2)

World Knowledge

### Your little brainiac.

Ask about virtually any topic, from quick dinner recipes to DIY home repairs to game-night trivia. Siri AI taps into broad world knowledge to give you highly specific, up-to-date answers.

Seamless Experience

### Carries the conversation.

Siri AI lets you ask open-ended questions and go back and forth with natural, rich conversations. And your conversations with Siri on HomeView sync to the Siri app on your other Apple devices so you can pick up where you left off.

### Multiple users.

HomeView can recognize up to six people and seamlessly switch between them. It syncs with each user’s iPhone or iPad and is designed to show their content only when they’re using HomeView.[12](https://www.apple.com/apple-homeview/#footnote-13)

![](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-homeview/a/images/overview/icons/secure_exclave__dodwizyj9maa_large.svg)

### Secure Exclaves.

The A18 chip inside HomeView contains Secure Exclaves that process the camera and microphone feeds in a secure compartment that cannot be accessed by the operating system or by Apple. This enables HomeView to recognize the face and voice of each user without exposing the audio and visual data used for recognition to the operating system.

![](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-homeview/a/images/overview/icons/personal_data__b9zqqmeo8gaa_large.svg)

### Personal content.

Users can access their personal content on HomeView when their paired iPhone or iPad is connected to the same Wi-Fi network and has been recently unlocked, and HomeView recognizes the person by face.

- HomeView is deeply integrated within the Apple ecosystem and features a stunning 7-inch Liquid Retina display and Apple‑engineered audio. Because multiple people can use HomeView, it is designed to keep everyone’s personal information separate.

  Users can access their personal content like Messages and Calendar on HomeView when their paired iPhone or iPad is connected to the same Wi-Fi network and has been recently unlocked, and HomeView recognizes the person by face.

  The A18 chip inside HomeView contains Secure Exclaves that process the camera and microphone feeds in a secure compartment that cannot be accessed by the operating system or by Apple. This enables HomeView to recognize the face and voice of each user without exposing the audio and visual data to the operating system.

- HomeView delivers room-filling sound with support for Spatial Audio, making it a great way to enjoy music and podcasts at home. Its custom-engineered acoustic system combines a down-firing woofer and two full-range drivers to produce deep bass and crisp highs. Powered by the A18 chip and built-in sensors, computational audio continuously optimizes the loudness and bass response at any volume.

  HomeView (Wall Mounted) brings full, balanced sound with two custom drivers and computational audio tuned specifically for on-wall placement, along with support for Spatial Audio.
