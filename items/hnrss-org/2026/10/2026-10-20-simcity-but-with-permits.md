## Build the city of your dreams.

Subject to approval

You are the mayor. You know exactly what this city needs. Now get it past the zoning board, the neighbors, the appeals court and the next election.

[New game](https://permit.petercai.com/play/) Free, because you are loved

Permit Pending is a city simulation in the spirit of the classics, with one difference: you are not a god. You are an elected mayor. Every apartment block, bus lane and factory goes through hearings, reviews, objections, lawsuits and appeals. The economy underneath is real. The neighbors are not always rational. Win the argument, or win the election. Ideally both.

## How a good idea dies

A typical case from your inbox. The economic answer was obvious. The political path was not.

1. Month 1 A tech boom pushes rents up. Young residents start to complain.
2. Month 3 You rezone an empty lot for a 12-story apartment building.
3. Month 4 **Filed.** Nearby homeowners organize over parking and "neighborhood character."
4. Month 7 **Hearing.** You trade height for parking. The building is now 6 stories.
5. Month 8 The smaller project no longer pays. The developer asks for a tax break.
6. Month 9 Housing advocates criticize the lower count of affordable units.
7. Month 11 **Appeal.** The board delays construction. A lawsuit is filed.
8. Month 42 Rents are still rising. Young voters blame you for the shortage. Homeowners blame you for the proposal.
9. Month 48 **Election day.**

### Every improvement creates opposition

New housing lowers rents and angers homeowners. New industry brings jobs and smog. Better transit attracts development, and development attracts opponents. Fix one problem and you create a new group of voters who are angry about it.

![The Case window of an upzoning at its planning hearing: 88% opposition, open objections and concessions the mayor can offer](https://permit.petercai.com/marketing/feature-hearing.jpg)

### Hearings, appeals and lawsuits

Your inbox fills with applications, controversies and recurring characters. Negotiate concessions at public hearings, win votes on a seven-seat council, and spend your political clout wisely. Some cases last longer than your term.

![Workshops, tank farms and warehouses on the same streets as small houses](https://permit.petercai.com/marketing/feature-industry.jpg)

### An economy that keeps score

Residents have ages, incomes and homes. Businesses hire, grow and leave. Land values, rents, jobs, taxes and services all connect. The city keeps changing while you are stuck in committee.
