Local Wikipedia is Wikipedia, but only for things a half hour walk from you.

Local content is direct from Wikipedia - geocoded pages only.

If there are fewer than 25 things close to you, it takes the closest 25 things within about 6 miles. If there are more than 500 things only the closest 500 will be included.

Your location stays in this browser: links you share don't include it.

This site has no affiliation with the actual Wikipedia.
