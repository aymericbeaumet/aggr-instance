## Introduction

If you’re just getting started with IRC development, the first thing to look at would be the IRC core specifications [`RFC1459`](https://tools.ietf.org/html/rfc1459) and [`RFC2812`](https://tools.ietf.org/html/rfc2812). One of our members has also been writing a new core protocol document [here](https://modern.ircdocs.horse), which you may find useful to consult. After that, our [specifications page](https://ircv3.net/irc/) contains the extensions the IRCv3 Working Group has developed.

All of the IRCv3 extensions are backwards-compatible with older IRC clients, and older IRC servers. Our [roadmap](https://github.com/ircv3/ircv3-specifications/milestone/4) details the specifications we have in the pipeline, and our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ircv3/ircv3-specifications) is where most of our specification work is done.

For any other questions, feel free to consult our [FAQ page](https://ircv3.net/faq) which contains all sorts of info about us and what we do.

If you’re interested in talking with us, our discussion channel is #ircv3 on Libera.Chat [\[webchat\]](https://kiwiirc.com/client/irc.libera.chat:+6697/#ircv3).

## IRCv3 Features

- Standardised account login using SASL to speed up registration and authentication. [\[3.1\]](https://ircv3.net/specs/extensions/sasl-3.1) [\[3.2\]](https://ircv3.net/specs/extensions/sasl-3.2)
- Providing the account information of other clients for the development of more advanced client features. [\[1\]](https://ircv3.net/specs/extensions/account-notify) [\[2\]](https://ircv3.net/specs/extensions/account-tag) [\[3\]](https://ircv3.net/specs/extensions/extended-join)
- Optional metadata able to be attached to each message for easier, standardised extension development. [\[link\]](https://ircv3.net/specs/extensions/message-tags)
- Instant away notifications, to let users know when other users go away or come back more quickly. [\[link\]](https://ircv3.net/specs/extensions/away-notify)
- Showing the actual time a message was received, improving history playback from IRC bouncers. [\[link\]](https://ircv3.net/specs/extensions/server-time)
- Grouping related messages to simplify collapsing and display of those messages to users. [\[link\]](https://ircv3.net/specs/extensions/batch)

## What We’re Working On

- Standardised account registration and verification, allowing clients to provide better interfaces for end users. [\[link\]](https://ircv3.net/specs/extensions/account-registration)
- Giving clients a standardised way to recognise, access and view chat history (provided by bouncers or servers). [\[link\]](https://ircv3.net/specs/batches/chathistory)
- Providing a mechanism to allow clients to automatically detect, move to, and keep using secure connections. [\[link\]](https://ircv3.net/specs/extensions/sts)
- Allowing Unicode nicknames and channel names, improving the chat experience for international users. [\[link\]](https://github.com/ircv3/ircv3-specifications/pull/272)
- Client avatars for display in graphical clients.
