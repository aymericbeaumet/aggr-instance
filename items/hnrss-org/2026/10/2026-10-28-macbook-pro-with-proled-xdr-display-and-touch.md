## Design

Everything to see here.

Introducing MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display. Featuring a groundbreaking tandem OLED display with Touch, Dynamic Island, a redesigned thermal system, an enhanced audio system, and slimmer display borders — all in a lighter design. It’s the pinnacle of pro.

PROLED XDR

Brilliance \
within reach.

PROLED XDR combines breakthrough tandem OLED technology with advanced algorithms in the display engine, delivering Extreme Dynamic Range and stunning visual fidelity for the ultimate front-of-screen performance. As a reference-caliber display, it delivers outstanding precision for filmmaking, design, and even medical imaging.[6](https://www.apple.com/macbook-pro/proled-xdr-display/#footnote-10) With unparalleled brightness and contrast, deeper blacks, and an exceptionally wide color gamut, you’ll immediately see why it’s the world’s best laptop display.

Strength in numbers.

Up to 1000 nits sustained full-screen brightness [4](https://www.apple.com/macbook-pro/proled-xdr-display/#footnote-8)

Up to 2000 nits peak HDR and SDR brightness

10,000,000:1 contrast ratio

True Tone technology

P3 + Adobe RGB wide color

ProMotion adaptive 10–120Hz refresh rate

## Touch

Magic on tap.

Thoughtfully designed for Mac, Touch introduces a whole new way to interact with your work. The Touch sensor is built directly into the display panel — not layered on top of it — reducing latency so you feel like you’re touching the content itself. Everyday experiences like scrolling and pinching to zoom feel natural and responsive. And new, intuitive interactions let you do even more, like manipulating 3D objects or playing virtual instruments.

### Scroll.

Move through content by sliding your finger across it.

### Zoom.

Pinch to zoom in on photos, documents, web pages, and more.

### Draw.

Sketch and annotate directly onscreen.

## Security

No compromises.

Security starts with Apple silicon and extends to the macOS architecture. This deep integration of hardware and software, along with automatic updates and built-in antivirus protection, helps keep MacBook Pro stable and secure for the long term. At the heart of MacBook Pro is the Secure Enclave, a dedicated security subsystem that’s separate from the main processor. It keeps your most sensitive data, including encryption keys and biometric information, fully isolated from the rest of the system — the same protection that powers Touch ID and Find My.
