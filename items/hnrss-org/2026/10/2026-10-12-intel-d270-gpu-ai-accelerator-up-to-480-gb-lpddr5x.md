Intel GPU D270 is an inference-optimized data center GPU designed for the agentic AI era. It is built to support enterprise AI, SaaS, GPUaaS, and government or sovereign-cloud deployments that need memory-rich, power-efficient AI acceleration.

Intel GPU D270 is designed for production AI inference workloads such as agentic AI, long-context retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), enterprise AI assistants, coding assistants, document understanding, multimodal AI, diffusion models, and high-concurrency serving.

Agentic AI often turns one task into multiple rounds of model calls, tool use, retrieval, reasoning, and response generation. Intel GPU D270 is designed for the inference requirements that become important in those environments, including prefill processing, larger context, memory capacity, concurrency, and power efficiency.

Inference-optimized means the GPU is designed for running AI models in production rather than training them at massive scale. Intel GPU D270 focuses on the needs of deployed AI applications, including prompt processing, high-concurrency serving, memory capacity, efficient power use, and system-level performance.

Long-context AI workloads can require more memory to support larger prompts, retrieval-heavy workflows, and KV cache growth during inference. Intel GPU D270 is designed with high available memory capacity to help support larger models, longer context, and more concurrent sessions.

Intel GPU D270 is designed around a standard PCIe 5.0 form factor and air-cooled deployment profile, helping enterprises evaluate AI inference acceleration within existing data center environments without requiring a specialized liquid-cooling retrofit.

The primary audience includes AI system architects, AI platform architects, infrastructure and cloud engineering leaders, enterprise AI decision-makers, SaaS providers, GPUaaS providers, government or sovereign-cloud teams, and developers deploying production inference workloads.

Customers should connect with Intel to learn more about Intel GPU D270, evaluate how it may fit their AI inference roadmap, and receive updates as additional product information becomes available.
