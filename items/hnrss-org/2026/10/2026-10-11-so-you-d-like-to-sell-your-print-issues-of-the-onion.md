If you are here, you are either simply a curious person or you believe you might have something of interest to us. If you are the former, stay curious! If you are the latter, keep on reading.

## How To Identify Your Issues.

To check whether you have the issues we’re seeking, look at the Volume and Issue Numbers. Every issue of *The Onion* has a volume number, an issue number, and a date. This information is all listed on the front page, but in different spots depending on the issue itself. Special issues may list the volume & issue numbers as “22-01” instead of “Volume 22 Issue 1”. See the examples below:

![Front page of The Onion, Volume 21, Issue 18, showing where the volume and issue numbers appear.](https://theonion.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/onion-vol21-issue18-front-page.jpg)\
Volume 21, Issue 18, June 16 to July 20, 1992.

![Front page of The Onion, Volume 32, Issue 4,. showing where the volume and issue numbers appear.](https://theonion.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/onion-vol32-issue4-front-page.jpg)\
Volume 32, Issue 4, August 28 to September 3, 1997.

## Have Some? Tell Us.

Let us know if you have any of the requested issues by completing the Google Form and providing as much information as you can. Our archivist will reach out to you if your collection is of interest to us. The more of our gaps you can fill, the more interested we will be.

**Note: The issues must be in good shape, clean, and readable. If you do sell an issue to us, it becomes property of *The Onion* and cannot be returned**

We will reach out with any additional questions if we have them.
