Astounding sound.

The mighty yet compact speakers pack a serious punch. Two custom full-range drivers in the display keep everything sounding crisp and clean while delivering room-filling Spatial Audio.4 Computational audio adjusts consistency and balance in real time to deliver incredible sound every time, from anywhere. And a down-firing woofer and two force-cancelling passive radiators are built into the base — for deep, rich bass.

Everything connected

and under one roof.

Apple HomeView is a central hub for your smart home accessories, making it easy to keep things running smoothly. And with the new Apple Home Collection, it’s never been simpler to set up and control a connected home.

Integrated with the Home app.

Built into HomeView and synced with your other Apple devices, the Home app keeps you on top of your smart home. See who’s at the door, monitor your entryway, access multiple video feeds, adjust the lighting and temperature, and control scenes and automations to fit the routines and rhythms of your household.11

See it all at a glance.

The Home Modular face gives you a clear and concise look at your home accessories, while the Home Camera face lets you stream up to nine videos at the same time. And you can manage it all with just your voice or a tap.

Enjoy peace of mind.

HomeView can detect the sounds of the smoke and carbon monoxide detectors in your home and alert you on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch.

Stay caught up.

The Home app uses Apple Intelligence to group related notifications together, such as when a family member arrives home, unlocks the door, and disables the alarm. And with descriptions of video footage, you can use HomeView to catch up on key activity in and around the house.

Multiple users.

HomeView can recognize up to six people and seamlessly switch between them. It syncs with each user’s iPhone or iPad and is designed to show their content only when they’re using HomeView.12

Secure Exclaves.

The A18 chip inside HomeView contains Secure Exclaves that process the camera and microphone feeds in a secure compartment that cannot be accessed by the operating system or by Apple. This enables HomeView to recognize the face and voice of each user without exposing the audio and visual data used for recognition to the operating system.

Personal content.

Users can access their personal content on HomeView when their paired iPhone or iPad is connected to the same Wi-Fi network and has been recently unlocked, and HomeView recognizes the person by face.
