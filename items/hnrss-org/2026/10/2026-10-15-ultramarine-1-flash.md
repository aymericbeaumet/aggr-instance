## Built for professional work

## Improved token efficiency

Across the six benchmarks, Ultramarine 1 Flash generates 6.5% fewer tokens than its base, GLM-5.3 Flash, taking a shorter path to better work.

Output tokens generated across all six benchmarks, reasoning included. Where GLM-5.3 Flash’s score is a public one, its tokens come from our own runs closest to that score.

## Fable-class model for cents per task

Ultramarine 1 Flash sits on the cost-quality frontier at the most attractive price point. At $0.13 per task, it outscores Claude Fable 5 (max) and GPT-5.6 Sol (max), at $8.19 and $2.09 per task, and comes within three points of Claude Opus 5.5 (max) and GPT-6 Sol (max) at $6.96 and $1.26 per task. We measure this on PI Knowledge Work, a carefully built internal benchmark whose scores correlate strongly with the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (r = 0.81).

PI Knowledge Work is our internal benchmark of professional work in finance, law, consulting, and operations. Its tasks ask the model to deliver finished Excel workbooks, PowerPoint decks, and Word documents, and to work inside live business systems such as ERP and MES.

A rubric written for each task scores the result on substance, accuracy, and presentation. Every model works in the same harness, and costs are at each developer's official API pricing. Read our [technical report](https://www.paperinstruments.com/blog/ultramarine) for more on the kind of professional data and evaluations that trained this model.

## Crafted with a sense of client readiness

Ultramarine 1 Flash writes clean, simple, straightforward prose and has a strong sense of visual and information hierarchy. It is built to adhere to the same rules required of analysts in professional services.
