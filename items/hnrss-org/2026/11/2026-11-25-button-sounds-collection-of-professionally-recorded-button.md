**How it works:** Click the big red button to play the currently-selected sound. There are more than 50 sounds to choose from in the “Select Sound” list; you can tap the up/down arrows to move between them.

Each sound has a handful of individually-recorded samples that are randomly selected on every tap and release. You can lock it to a specific sample by tapping the numbers towards the bottom of the demo.

**Not hearing anything?** Frustratingly, browsers can decide to block a website from playing any sound. iOS Safari seems particularly strict about this. So, if you’re not hearing a click sound when tapping the big red button, please check out this website on a desktop computer.
