## [Researchers Report Direct Edits to Neural-Network Weights](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/)

An MIT CSAIL team reports early experiments in which GPT-6 Astra interpreted and modified neural-network weights without writing a training loop. In one Qwen test, the authors say a small weight change sharply improved math and instruction-following scores; the findings are preliminary.

[![The authors’ diagram of experiments reading, repairing and improving neural-network weights](https://rikiyat.github.io/images/blog/weight-space-programming.png)](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/)\
Rikiya Takehi and colleagues / MIT CSAIL

## [Andrej Karpathy Builds a Video Library From Hacker News Recommendations](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/hacker-tube/)

HackerTube gathers 6,454 YouTube videos mentioned by Hacker News readers across nearly two decades. Karpathy describes using AI coding tools to build an alternative browsing experience, with its own recommendations and no user accounts.

[![HackerTube’s video-browsing interface, shown in Andrej Karpathy’s blog post](https://bear-images.sfo2.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/karpathy/image-2.webp)](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/hacker-tube/)\
Screenshot via Andrej Karpathy

## [PostgreSQL Developer Links Release Delay to AI-Found Bugs](http://rhaas.blogspot.com/2026/10/postgres-19-is-late-and-its-ais-fault.html)

In an October 31 article, freenode news reported that Ubuntu’s 22.04, 24.04 and 26.04 LTS releases still lacked kernels patched for CVE-2026-98432. This is the report’s account, not a fresh verification of Canonical’s current patch status.

## [An Argument for Mathematics Beyond Individual Credit](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/11/02/egoless-mathematics-in-the-ai-era/)

In a guest essay on Terence Tao’s blog, Karthik C. S., Amit Sahai and Jie Xue argue that mathematical work should be valued for the understanding it brings, not for outperforming other people or machines. They urge a less ego-centered response to AI’s role in research.

## [Central Warsaw, Rebuilt One Voxel at a Time](https://p.migdal.pl/warsaw-vibe-voxel/)

The project presents a three-dimensional model of central Warsaw assembled from OpenStreetMap, LiDAR and the city’s tree database, with a voxel resolution of one meter.

## [A Backstage Glimpse of the Windows 2000 Launch](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/oldnewthing/20261102-00/?p=112818/)

In a Microsoft blog recollection, the author describes slipping into an early rehearsal and learning that Patrick Stewart would host the Windows 2000 launch. A headset-wearing crew member stood in for the actor during the run-through.
