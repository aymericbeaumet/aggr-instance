We are proud to announce something that our small team of very dedicated people at Kagi has been building for a long time – the **public beta of Kagi Mail**. A private email and calendar with a @kagimail.com address, or with your own domains.

If you’ve already swapped Google Search for Kagi and Chrome for Orion, your inbox is probably the last big piece of your digital life still at Google. Now you can move that too.

## Why we built it

*Does the world need another email service?* We asked ourselves that question more than once. There are great ones out there, and we’re fans of what Fastmail and Tuta are doing. We still built our own, for the same reason we build everything at Kagi: **technology should do its job and then get out of your way**.

For email, that job is to be fast, private, and secure. On top of that, it should help you stay on track with what’s important: bring forward what you need to act on, respond to, or acknowledge, and quietly handle the rest.

[Kagi Mail](https://kagi.com/products/mail) also has to treat you the way Kagi Search and Orion do: no surveillance, no ads, open standards, and no lock-in.

**Already a Kagi member?**

Kagi Mail is included in **Ultimate**. Sign in with your Kagi account, and you’re set. AI features draw from your existing Allowance. On another plan, or no Kagi account yet? After the free 60-day trial, you can get Kagi Mail for $99/year (+ Sales Tax).

## How it works: A service and a client, on open standards

To deliver the experience we believe in, we built the full stack: hosting for your addresses and domains, plus the mail and calendar apps. It runs on open standards and open-source infrastructure (the Stalwart server, IMAP, JMAP, and CalDAV), so you can use Kagi’s apps or any compatible client you already like.

### The basics

- A @kagimail.com address
- Unlimited aliases
- Unlimited domains
- Built-in Calendar
- 50 GB of storage

### Intelligence

- **Organize your mail in your own words.** Brief it the way you’d brief an assistant: “Label anything where beta users send product feedback. It might come from @kagimail.com, but use judgment.” You decide what permissions each rule gets (read the content, or skip the inbox).
- **Inbox Highlights** show what needs you today, and learn from what you dismiss.
- **Ask your inbox** in plain language and get direct answers with the source email, including text inside scanned PDFs and photos.
- **Newsletter view** reads newsletters like a feed, out of the way of real mail.
- **Responsible AI, with one switch to turn it all off.** You control [what AI is allowed to do and infer](https://help.kagi.com/kagi/why-kagi/ai-philosophy.html).

### Private by default

- Tracking pixels and remote images are blocked or proxied, so senders can’t tell when or where you opened their mail.
- Sender screening holds new senders until you approve them, so they never reach your inbox uninvited.
- Suspicious senders trigger a phishing warning, while verified senders show their (BMI) logo.

**No ads, ever. No profiling, no selling, no sharing your data.**

### For teams and families

- **Shared addresses on your domain** (team@, info@, family@): create an alias, then choose which people have access to it
- Sign in through a social login, or your organization’s own identity provider (OIDC)
- **Coming soon:** Shared mailboxes

### Kagi integration

- Kagi Translate is built in
- **Email Assistant:** Write to assistant@kagimail.com for answers about your mail (actions coming soon)
- Proofread and translation included while you write emails

### For power users and developers

- **Keyboard-first:** command palette, shortcuts, and search operators
- Rule-based filtering with Sieve, no AI involved
- MCP server with read access to your mail
- Works with Apple Mail, Thunderbird, and any IMAP/JMAP/CalDAV client, with automatic setup

### What’s not here yet

We will keep adding new capabilities – here’s what to expect over the next few quarters.

| Feature                         | Target  |
| ------------------------------- | ------- |
| Contacts and address book       | Q1 2027 |
| Full Kagi Assistant integration | Q1 2027 |
| Native mobile apps              | Q3 2027 |

Until the mobile apps arrive, use the mobile web app or any IMAP/JMAP client, such as Apple Mail or Thunderbird.

## Our “no lock-in” promise to you

Leaving Kagi Mail is as easy as joining it. Your mail, calendar, and workflows are yours, and you can export them at any time, through standard protocols and a one-click export experience. You’ll keep access to your mailbox for six months after leaving Kagi Mail. You can send and receive emails for the first 60 days, then read and export your mail with one click for the remaining four months. **We build an ecosystem so things work better together for those who want it, not to trap anyone**.

**We want Kagi Mail to be the first email account we’d create for our children.** We’re not there yet, but it is a high bar we hold ourselves to, and every feature in this beta is a step toward it: a calm, safe place to communicate, and a window onto an internet that treats you as a person, not a product who’s been tracked, targeted, and sold to.

**We can build it this way because of you.** Kagi has no ad business, and no one pushing us to monetize your inbox; you’re not a dataset for ads or AI. You are here because you believe the internet can be built differently. Thank you.
