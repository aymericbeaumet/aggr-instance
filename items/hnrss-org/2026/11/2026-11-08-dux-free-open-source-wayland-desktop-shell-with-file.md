Dux Modular Shell automatically generates matching colors for supported GTK and Qt apps, terminals, editors, and more.

Our custom [dank16](https://duxlabs.dev/docs/dms/cli-dank16) algorithm builds a full 16-color palette for terminals and syntax highlighting, with contrast tuned for light and dark themes.

- GTK3, GTK4, and Qt
- Terminal and editor color schemes
- Light and dark modes

[Set up app theming](https://duxlabs.dev/docs/dms/application-themes)
