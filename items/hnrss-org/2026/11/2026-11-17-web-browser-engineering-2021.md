Web browsers are ubiquitous, but how do they work? This book explains, building a basic but complete web browser, from networking to JavaScript, in a couple thousand lines of Python.

Follow this book’s [blog](https://browserbook.substack.com/archive), [Mastodon](https://indieweb.social/@browserbook), or [Twitter](https://twitter.com/browserbook) for updates. There’s a [discussion forum](https://github.com/browserengineering/book/discussions) for the book on Github, or you can [email us directly](mailto:author@browser.engineering).
