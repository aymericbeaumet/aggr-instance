## 1\. Commercial Platform & Services

Global Commerce Graph is a commercial intelligence platform. It synthesizes publicly available web data (company websites, business registries, public directories, and the open web) into a searchable graph of companies, the people associated with them, and the commercial relationships between them. The platform then computes alignment between businesses to estimate how well they could work together as customers, partners, or vendors.

The platform delivers this intelligence through Graph Walkers, automated research agents that walk the public web on a schedule, and delivers the resulting target buyers and referral partners to your workspace via a daily webhook at 6:00 AM Eastern Time. Graph data is also available through MCP and API endpoints for integration into your own tools, and through ICP & Geometric Fit Checks that score how closely a business matches your ideal customer profile.

Everything the platform produces (fit scores, account recommendations, audience maps, and drafted content) is a probabilistic model built from public commercial data. It is a decision-support tool, not a guarantee. You are responsible for evaluating the underlying public data and the accuracy of any output before you take commercial action on it, including reaching out to a company, pitching a partnership, or making purchasing decisions.

## 2\. Subscriptions, Compute & Billing

Access to Global Commerce Graph is provided through monthly recurring subscription plans. Plans renew automatically each billing period unless canceled. Compute is consumed the moment it executes: crawling, indexing, vector search, and AI synthesis incur immediate, non-recoverable infrastructure costs. Subscription fees are therefore non-refundable once a billing cycle has processed.

You can cancel at any time through your account; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades take effect at the conclusion of the active billing period. If you believe a charge is the result of a billing error, contact us at [hello@globalcommercegraph.com](mailto:hello@globalcommercegraph.com) and we will review it.

## 3\. Permitted B2B Outreach & Compliance

Global Commerce Graph does not send outreach on your behalf or without your explicit configuration. Any campaign or message that goes out under your account is something you set up and control. You retain full legal responsibility for any outreach, email, telephone, or marketing communications you initiate using the service, and you must comply with the CAN-SPAM Act, the Canadian Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), and all other applicable marketing and privacy laws in the jurisdictions you contact. That includes honoring opt-out requests, using accurate sender identification, and not misrepresenting who you are or why you are reaching out.

Our indexing of publicly available business data is conducted under the legitimate interest basis of GDPR Article 6(1)(f): mapping corporate and market ecosystems is a legitimate interest of our business and of the businesses that use the service to find partners and customers.

## 4\. Data Collection & Privacy

We collect the account data needed to operate your workspace: your work email, billing history processed by Stripe on our behalf, authentication credentials, and workspace settings. The graph itself is built from public business information: public enterprise domains and company registry data, publicly published leadership and team pages, Common Crawl web links, and publicly indexed page relationships.

We do not harvest consumer data, do not collect or index private consumer health records, financial account credentials, sensitive personal data, or data of minors, and we do not sell personal information or trade contact lists. Your information is shared only with the sub-processors required to operate the service, under strict data-protection agreements.

Any company or representative who wants their public domain, company profile, or contact information removed or permanently suppressed from our graph search index can email [hello@globalcommercegraph.com](mailto:hello@globalcommercegraph.com) with their domain. We honor all suppression requests promptly.

## 5\. Data Security & Contact

We use strictly essential session cookies and local storage tokens for authentication and rate-limiting quotas. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers, including Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, or third-party ad retargeting networks.

For all privacy questions, suppression requests, or data deletion inquiries, contact us at [hello@globalcommercegraph.com](mailto:hello@globalcommercegraph.com). We will respond to verified requests promptly.
