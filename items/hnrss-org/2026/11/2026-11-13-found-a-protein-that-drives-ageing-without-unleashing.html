Aged mice engineered to lack granzyme K have improved heart and liver function and less kidney damage.

Researchers have found an enzyme that drives ageing in the body’s tissues1. Drugs that reduce the levels of this protein might one day combat the effects of ageing without impairing the body’s immune defences.
