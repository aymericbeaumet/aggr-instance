### background

in 2013 i founded ArtSpot. our vision was to give New Yorkers a bit of joy by exposing them to local art. we did this through informal agreements with dozens of cafes, small galleries, and restaurants. "*let us curate art on your walls, get paid if someone buys it.*"

and it worked. sort of. finding artists was easy, building the platform was easy. getting permission from venues was slow but straightforward. the problem was nobody actually bought the art. i remember the admin email notification that first time a piece was sold. it was a small painting of Janelle Monáe. the buyer was my friend, a gift to me. here it is, still in my possession.

anyway ArtSpot failed. five years later i paid back my investors (+ 13% interest). but the vision never went away. i love making and consuming art.

### opinionated platform

i probably say "*the most popular feature request since launch is \_\_\_\_\_\_*" too often. after saying no (and yes) to so many requests, it's tough to keep count.

but with certainty, one of our strongest convictions was about color. TRMNL is not a television. it is not a clock. we avoid trying to do too much.

then [grayscale happened](https://trmnl.com/blog/no-more-flicker). photos look great. art is possible. community member Clark debuted *Jaws* (yes the movie), [1 frame at a time](https://x.com/clarklab/status/1921209108377788721).

![](https://trmnl.com/rails/active_storage/representations/proxy/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6MTk4Mjk4MDQsInB1ciI6ImJsb2JfaWQifX0=--5e58d212fcdb23115dadb790b3df805e545f380a/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6eyJmb3JtYXQiOiJwbmciLCJyZXNpemVfdG9fbGltaXQiOlsxMDI0LDc2OF19LCJwdXIiOiJ2YXJpYXRpb24ifX0=--a1b29f018116e3f4abd0440beb02228a54f47dd1/image.png)\

which got us thinking... maybe we were wrong about color. maybe it aligns with our vision and just maybe, it makes it even bigger.

### introducing Frame

today i'm thrilled to bury the lede and announce [Frame](https://shop.trmnl.com/products/frame). it's a 31.5 inch full color ePaper display, enclosed in a hand made wooden body that we manufacture ourselves. yes, we opened our own woodworking shop a few miles from HQ. a closer look at the workshop is coming soon.

![](https://trmnl.com/rails/active_storage/representations/proxy/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6MTk4MzE2MzksInB1ciI6ImJsb2JfaWQifX0=--0043b9a63057a343579da0458a8efdeea5ad024c/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6eyJmb3JtYXQiOiJqcGciLCJyZXNpemVfdG9fbGltaXQiOlsxMDI0LDc2OF19LCJwdXIiOiJ2YXJpYXRpb24ifX0=--d5e667c0137ab726f37f5f3b8ccaf980246e425b/trmnl-frame-on-kickstand.jpg)\
 Schedule + Weather + Art in 1 screen

Frame gets all the same features as other TRMNL devices, with a few additional perks spanning both the web platform and the hardware itself. [watch the short film](https://youtu.be/RmoSjWDiMEE) or read along below.

**Clock Sync**

often requested, always ignored, until now. Frame will update at specified moments of the day versus a relative "every N minutes" cadence. this reduces screen flicker while also extending the battery life and adding continuity to your schedule.

![](https://trmnl.com/rails/active_storage/representations/proxy/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6MTk4Mjk4NDYsInB1ciI6ImJsb2JfaWQifX0=--6ff2fbc655849fe1250ae91bf918c179bce90f0b/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6eyJmb3JtYXQiOiJwbmciLCJyZXNpemVfdG9fbGltaXQiOlsxMDI0LDc2OF19LCJwdXIiOiJ2YXJpYXRpb24ifX0=--a1b29f018116e3f4abd0440beb02228a54f47dd1/trmnl-clock-sync.png)

**Offline Mode**

![](https://trmnl.com/rails/active_storage/representations/proxy/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6MTk4MzA1NTUsInB1ciI6ImJsb2JfaWQifX0=--4aafffa109faf77b4a2dc8409acca63562a3e848/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6eyJmb3JtYXQiOiJqcGciLCJyZXNpemVfdG9fbGltaXQiOlsxMDI0LDc2OF19LCJwdXIiOiJ2YXJpYXRpb24ifX0=--d5e667c0137ab726f37f5f3b8ccaf980246e425b/trmnl-frame-offline-mode.jpg)\

the internet is cool but totally unnecessary. Frame's microSD port lets you run devices completely offline with zero personal information reaching our servers.

**Dedicated Power (optional)**

![](https://trmnl.com/rails/active_storage/representations/proxy/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6MTk4MzA1NTMsInB1ciI6ImJsb2JfaWQifX0=--1dc809aa3091e0ee7f8b70d796bf086aebb34bd1/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6eyJmb3JtYXQiOiJqcGciLCJyZXNpemVfdG9fbGltaXQiOlsxMDI0LDc2OF19LCJwdXIiOiJ2YXJpYXRpb24ifX0=--d5e667c0137ab726f37f5f3b8ccaf980246e425b/trmnl-frame-back-usb-port.jpg)\

like all TRMNL devices, a rechargeable battery is included (10-20k mAh available). but with Frame we added an extra USB-C port on the rear of the device, accessible with a small hole through the back panel for always-on connectivity.

**Color Expertise**

![](https://trmnl.com/rails/active_storage/representations/proxy/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6MTk4MzAwNjQsInB1ciI6ImJsb2JfaWQifX0=--a9188b20fe76b8daa335b3ed3fab7c4b4b194604/eyJfcmFpbHMiOnsiZGF0YSI6eyJmb3JtYXQiOiJqcGVnIiwicmVzaXplX3RvX2xpbWl0IjpbMTAyNCw3NjhdfSwicHVyIjoidmFyaWF0aW9uIn19--b5fc5af83c336b0fe3eb199eed01a2c85b3a702d/trmnl-lazarus-workbench.jpeg)\
 Lazarus workbench

some of my colleagues are snobs about ePaper dithering algorithms and LUTs (look-up tables). this is now your gain.

over the past few months we've been developing Lazarus, a color conversion engine that we think provides Frame the best color matching on the market for any Spectra 6 display. it's also coming soon to our [4-color device](https://shop.trmnl.com/products/trmnl-bwry), and yes it will be open source for your own tuning pleasure.

### transparent supply chain

before TRMNL i spent 10 years in b2b software and knew nothing about making physical products for everyday people. nobody ever blasted me on reddit about injection mold flow lines or damaged UPS packages. at best i'd get a G2 review every 3 months.

this all changed in [July 2024](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/usetrmnl/trmnl-the-e-ink-display-for-your-favorite-apps-and-news) and after shipping 60,000 devices to 79 countries we've gotten pickier about operational design. chief among these new requirements is doing as much production as possible in-house.

here are Frame's components and where they're sourced:

| **Component**                      | **Location** | **Lead Times**                                                                   | **Inventory** |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Overseas                           | 2-3 months   | 300 in warehouse, 200 en route                                                   |               |
| Alabama, USA                       | 1-2 months   | samples received, design tweaks sent for larger batch                            |               |
| TRMNL shop, Pine Mountain, Georgia | 1 week       | made-to-order pending wood species chosen at checkout                            |               |
| Overseas                           | 3-4 weeks    | 500 in warehouse, includes American + European style adapters                    |               |
| TRMNL HQ, LaGrange, Georgia        | 1-2 days     | made daily from a growing fleet of 3D printers                                   |               |
| Overseas                           | 2-3 weeks    | 1,000 full assemblies (raw components) in warehouse, bulk pick & place order TBD |               |
| Overseas + USA                     | 2-3 weeks    | 500 full assemblies ordered, en route next week                                  |               |
| Overseas + USA                     | 1 week       | 50 in warehouse, 450 en route                                                    |               |
| Overseas                           | 2-3 weeks    | Samples in warehouse, 800 ordered, en route next week                            |               |

by reducing complexity, [learning from](https://trmnl.com/blog/on-x) past releases, and having everything we need either in stock or on order, i expect this to be our smoothest fulfillment cycle yet. as with other devices, all Frames are assembled by our own team and never outsourced.

### logistics

Batch #1 is available to customers in locations our USA warehouse already ships to: USA, Canada, Mexico, Australia. [pre-order here](https://shop.trmnl.com/products/frame).

we're open to shipping elsewhere, but Duty / Import fees could be quite high given the product's Customs value. **to participate in Batch #1 as a resident outside these countries**, [**go here**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc5oFPs4TqFvyo13ipocjO5n1eK545V58Q8_CwD8oVSXy0bRQ/viewform?usp=publish-editor). this option may be de-activated without notice if inventory sells out.
