Motorola’s best camera system yet, recognized for imaging excellence

The motorola signature 27 includes the world’s best camera sytsem launched in 202625, combining advanced hardware, cinematic video, and effortless photography. Awarded the coveted DXOMARK Gold Label in recognition of its industry-leading level of imaging quality performance and outranking all other Android and iOS devices, the motorola signature 27’s camera system delivers eye-catching photos and videos, with:

Combined with LOFIC technology, it records brighter, more balanced video with consistent detail across challenging lighting conditions, whether filming concert stages, neon-lit streets, candlelit dinners or nights out with friends.

The 3x periscope telephoto lens also delivers flattering, natural-looking portraits, while ultra-resolution imaging provides greater flexibility for cropping, reframing, and editing.

Working together with the Action Shot feature, it freezes motion while maximizing available light, producing crisp, vibrant images with richer detail and more natural color.
  • The 50MP ultrawide camera³ fits more into every frame, transforming tight spaces into expansive compositions and making it easier to capture sweeping landscapes, large landmarks and group photos without stepping back.
  • And a 50MP front camera delivers sharp, ready-to-share selfies and supports 4K video recording, so selfie videos feature the same bold colors and clarity as still photos.

For a more cinematic video experience, the motorola signature 27 features a powerful Video Enhancement Engine that uses AI to intelligently optimize color, contrast and dynamic range in real time. Support for Dolby Vision®, the same technology used by leading filmmakers, helps enable richer, more immersive video. Combined with the Qualcomm® Electronic Image Stabilization and 3.5° OIS hardware on the main camera, panning, walking, and handheld recording looks more fluid and stable, so users can stay in the moment while the camera does the hard work.

AI that goes beyond assistance

The motorola signature 27 is designed for Gemini Intelligence, which brings the best of Gemini to Android’s most advanced devices, alongside out-of-box Google Gemini experiences and Motorola Qira.

The motorola signature 27 can understand context, help people create, support decisions and move from intention to action across the apps and services they use every day. As Google rolls out these capabilities to Gemini Intelligence⁷ devices globally in the coming months, motorola signature 27 will be ready to bring them to users around the world:

  • Whether booking a ride or preparing a grocery order from a shopping list on your screen, Gemini helps users go from intention to action with less effort.  Gemini can handle complex, multi-step tasks on a user’s behalf, reducing app switching and helping them stay focused on what matters. Users remain in control throughout, with the ability to view progress, jump in, or stop a task.16
  • Rambler17 transforms natural speech into polished messages, letting users think out loud while it refines their words in the background. Rambler removes filler words, edits repetition, and organizes ideas into clear paragraphs or bullet points. Users can correct themselves mid-sentence, switch languages seamlessly, and review everything before sending, ensuring they share exactly what they intend. Afterwards, users can quickly ask Rambler to rewrite, rephrase, translate, or reformat their text.
  • Create My Widget18 enables users to create personalized widgets by simply describing what they want to see. Whether it’s a tracker to follow your monthly spending, daily recipe inspiration, or a trip countdown with your travel details, your homescreen can be as personalized as you want it to be.
  • Sign-to-Text19 translates American Sign Language (ASL) into written English in real time using the device’s front-facing camera. Integrated with Gboard and Live Transcribe, it opens new ways for users to communicate across digital and in-person interactions.

Right out of the box, the motorola signature 27 also includes powerful Google Gemini⁸ experiences designed to bring new levels of creativity, including Gemini Omni, which enables users to create and edit video through natural conversation, and Lyria which generates custom soundtracks from simple prompts.

Motorola’s first GrapheneOS-supported device, built for privacy and peace of mind

Data privacy matters, and Motorola continues to build on its commitment to security with ThinkShield®, which helps protect users from the factory to the phone, and Moto Secure, a centralized destination for managing privacy and security features.

Expanding that commitment even further, the motorola signature 27 will kickstart a new era of smartphone privacy and security. It will be Motorola’s first device to support GrapheneOS, a hardened, open-source operating system based on the Android Open Source Project. Developed in partnership with the GrapheneOS Foundation, the leading nonprofit in advanced mobile security, the device reflects Motorola’s commitment to bringing cutting-edge security to everyday users across the globe.

That commitment runs deeper than software: GrapheneOS sets demanding requirements for the hardware it runs on and how it’s kept updated, and the motorola signature 27 is built to meet them. The device is Motorola’s first with a performant implementation of ARM Memory Tagging Extension, a new hardware caching mechanism for helping ensure system integrity and privacy. More details on it will be shared in the coming months.
