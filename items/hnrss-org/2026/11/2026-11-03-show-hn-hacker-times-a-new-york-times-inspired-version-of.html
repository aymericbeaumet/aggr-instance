Researchers Report Direct Edits to Neural-Network Weights

An MIT CSAIL team reports early experiments in which GPT-6 Astra interpreted and modified neural-network weights without writing a training loop. In one Qwen test, the authors say a small weight change sharply improved math and instruction-following scores; the findings are preliminary.

Andrej Karpathy Builds a Video Library From Hacker News Recommendations

HackerTube gathers 6,454 YouTube videos mentioned by Hacker News readers across nearly two decades. Karpathy describes using AI coding tools to build an alternative browsing experience, with its own recommendations and no user accounts.

An Argument for Mathematics Beyond Individual Credit

In a guest essay on Terence Tao’s blog, Karthik C. S., Amit Sahai and Jie Xue argue that mathematical work should be valued for the understanding it brings, not for outperforming other people or machines. They urge a less ego-centered response to AI’s role in research.

The project presents a three-dimensional model of central Warsaw assembled from OpenStreetMap, LiDAR and the city’s tree database, with a voxel resolution of one meter.
