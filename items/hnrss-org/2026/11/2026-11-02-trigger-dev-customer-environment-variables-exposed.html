There has been a security incident at Trigger.dev.

We've blocked the exploit used in this attack, but our investigation has identified exposure across a subset of organizations.

The attacker gained unauthorized access to environment variables for these organizations.

We have emailed all impacted account owners with steps to mitigate.

We will publish updates on our status page, and you can contact us if you have any questions.
