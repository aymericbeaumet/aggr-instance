The Apple Support Community will permanently close on November 20, 2026. Starting today, the site will no longer accept new posts and is available in a read-only state.

You can continue to find trusted guidance and support on the official [Apple Support site](http://support.apple.com).

We want to thank the community members who have contributed their time and expertise to help others with their Apple products.

Find answers. Ask questions. Connect with Apple customers around the world.

[Learn more about the Apple Support Community](https://discussions.apple.com/learn)
