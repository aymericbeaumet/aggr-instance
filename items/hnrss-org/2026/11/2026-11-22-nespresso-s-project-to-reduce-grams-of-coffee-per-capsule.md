## Introduction

The article is about Nespresso’s internal project to basically reduce the amount of grams per capsule that they produce. In August of 2026, I was informed by a Nespresso internal employee about “Project Olympus”. At first I didn’t think much of it and wasn’t sure how to deal with the information. It seems based on the docs that they were possibly improving the formulas, but I still questioned how that is possible with a reduction of grams of coffee.

## The Documentation

The below text is the actual Project Olympus internal text for everyone’s reference to review:

PROJECT OLYMPUS: VERTUO\
As part of our ongoing commitment to delivering an exceptional coffee experience, Nespresso is continuously innovating across every step of our process-while preserving the taste, quality, and consistency our consumers expect.

What’s changing?

Beginning in 2026, select Nespresso Vertuo coffee recipes will undergo a recipe optimization. Through advancements in grinding and roasting techniques, we’ve refined these recipes to achieve the same great taste and in-cup experience—using slightly less coffee per capsule.

This change will be rolled out gradually from Q1 through Q3 2026.

What’s staying the same?

While the capsule weight may be slightly reduced, the core elements of the coffee experience are unchanged:

- Same taste profile consumers know and love
- Same coffee volume per cup
- Same caffeine levels, with only minor natural variation
- Same high-quality standards across all affected coffees

These updates have been carefully validated from a sensory perspective to ensure no compromise in flavor or satisfaction.

Why are we optimizing recipes?

Innovation is at the heart of Nespresso. Our development teams are constantly exploring ways to improve how we roast and grind our coffees-while delivering the same consistent, high-quality results in every cup.

Project Olympus reflects this effort: a behind-the-scenes optimization designed to maintain excellence, even as we evolve our processes.

What if consumers ask questions?

In some cases, consumers may notice that certain capsules feel lighter than before or ask about possible changes. If inquiries arise, teams should emphasize:

- Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction
- That the taste and experience remain the same
- That the update reflects process innovation, not a reduction in value\
  The approved reactive statement and Q&A from the Project Olympus FAQ should be used to guide responses.
- HQ development teams are continuously looking into innovating our coffee processes, whilst ensuring optimal and consistent in-cup quality.
- We have optimized the grinding and roasting processes of some of our coffees, which has resulted to a slightly reduced capsule weight while providing the same consumer experience coffee lovers expect from Nespresso.

WAVE 1: Projected transition in market Q1 2026

- Scope (6 SKUS): DOUBLE ESPRESSO SCURO, DOUBLE ESPRESSO CHIARO, DOUBLE ESPRESSO DOLCE, FORTADO, ORAFIO, RISTRETTO INTENSO

WAVE 2: Projected transition in market TBA

- Scope (8 SKUS): MELOZIO, STORMIO, ODACIO, MO COSTA RICA, MO COLOMBIA, BIANCO DOPPIO, SOLEILIO, ARONDIO

Review attached memo and FAQ for additional information. No action is required as of right now.

## Officially Whistle Blowing

On/about Sepetember 5, 2026, I noticed a post on [r/nespresso](https://www.reddit.com/r/nespresso/) asking about whether Nespresso had changed their recipes:

[I then decided to offically be the first whistle blower](https://www.reddit.com/r/nespresso/comments/1w8d4lp/comment/p82fbrc/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=web3x&utm_name=web3xcss&utm_term=1&utm_content=share_button) about Project Olympus. A large number of Nespresso consumers were definitely not happy with Nespresso’s decision to reduce the number of grams per capsule. There are many users that are seriously considering to drop Nespresso and move to alternative coffee options.

## Coffee Capsules Affected

There are a number of people that have asked which coffees are affected, and that’s a resonable question. From what is currently being observed, almost all the range of coffees are being reduced. The only ones that don’t appear to be affected are Lungos for OL, and Alto XLs (and other larger coffees) for Vertuo.
