Google is sabotaging their OEM partners by making a growing number of features and updates exclusive to Pixels. At the same time, Google is trying to convince OEM partners not to support GrapheneOS or even bootloader unlocking. They're also trying to harm GrapheneOS directly.

There are a lot of engineers at Google who support what we're building and have directly worked with us. People there have repeatedly helped us and even explicitly added support for GrapheneOS to Google software including supporting Vanadium for Play services FIDO2 and passkeys.

FIDO2 and passkeys can also be used without Google Play. We had a workaround to make it work with Google Play and could have worked around the other stuff too but it's nice they implemented this. Google's engineers are not the problem. Their business people are the problem.

Google's business side is actively trying to wipe out alternatives. Google Mobile Services licensing has many illegal terms aimed at preventing competition. That isn't enough for them. They're very directly trying to harm GrapheneOS in multiple ways and outright wipe it out.

The current situation is serious. We're very interested in talking to @AGToddBlanche and others in a position to take rapid action against Google's escalating anti-competitive behavior. They're taking increasingly extreme actions to wipe out alternatives and action is needed.

There have been multiple lawsuits by the US DoJ against Google for antitrust violations with Android. Those have barely touched on the most egregious criminal antitrust violations which are currently rapidly escalating. We're interested in talking to @TheJusticeDept about it.

We expect Google will begin bootloader locking Pixels, continue heavily pushing the illegal Play Integrity API and trying to make forks of AOSP non-viable. Many of their actions are clear violation of US criminal law. US antitrust law even includes prison time for individuals.

Recent actions by Google have made it clear they're actively at war against alternatives. Our community can help us get in touch with government prosecutors and regulators. We have the resources, support and tools to continue regardless of Google's actions but won't do nothing.

Google has been repeatedly reaching out to OEMs to question whether they're working with us and threaten them based on GMS licensing terms found to be illegal by multiple courts. They're trying to stop us from having more than Pixels to support. It's completely outrageous.
