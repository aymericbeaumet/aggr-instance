The Majestic Million ranks domains based on the number of different subnets linking to them.[^5] This domain is the fastest growing in the top 100.

Another, maybe unrelated anomaly I saw is ailawandorder.com (registered in 2023) at rank 80, whose “About Us” page says its CEO is serving as a Board Member at Majestic. This made me check rank 800 in case there was a pattern, and no, there isn’t, that’s nsw.gov.au.

Before I got rate limited from Majestic, I opened Site Explorer, and it told me w2rt6byl5nf1opmc-a.com has 241,085,378 nofollow backlinks and 6 follow backlinks split across 5,163,708 referring domains. Checking Ahrefs, I see just [53 backlinks](https://ahrefs.com/backlink-checker/?input=w2rt6byl5nf1opmc-a.com&mode=subdomains); they read like the ones I’ve been getting since [The Article](https://jack.cab/blog/every-firefox-extension) fell off.

All the backlinks on Majestic are “fresh”, which means in the last 120 days. In fact, the domain was bought (not by me 💔) for the first time (according to Certificate Transparency) on November 11, *after* most of the backlinks. I found [an AI-generated PR from 2 weeks ago](https://github.com/zephium-browser/Zephium/pull/385) referencing the domain, and Tranco has data from October 5.

Majestic has indexed 1,267 pages on this domain and crawled 27. The site isn’t on the Wayback Machine so I can’t collect its pages.

In the Anchor Text table, the link count adds up to nine. There isn’t an “other” section. Are the remaining 240 million links images? No, they aren’t, it says the links are 100% TextLinks.

Then I downloaded Majestic Million myself and searched for the domain. There were six subdomains.

## Theories

I could probably figure out what’s happening here in 30 seconds by buying a Majestic subscription but I’m lazy.

- *Copyright trap*: maybe it’s fake, injected into the list by Majestic so they can discover who’s using their list. Could w2rt6byl5nf1opmc-a.com and ailawandorder.com be in the top 100 for this reason? Probably not, because if they cared, they’d relicense the list away from CC-BY. Also, the domains are ranking higher in Majestic over time instead of staying static.
- *Majestic got hacked*, and the hackers for some reason decided to tamper with the DB like this. **No.** [Are you stupid, are you dumb, are you Dario Amodei, are you all three](https://www.tiktok.com/@baghdxddy/video/7694814395293027615)‽
- *Some loophole/gaming the rankings*: Maybe “subnets” is counted in a stupid way for [IPv6](https://github.com/blechschmidt/freebind). Maybe the millions of domains are ip6.arpa. Majestic counts Class C subnets.
- *A bunch of hacked websites linking to the domain*: Seems unlikely, considering I’m unable to find a single backlink in the wild that wouldn’t be there for say, my website, and Ahrefs only has 53 backlinks for this domain. They could be [cloaking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloaking).
