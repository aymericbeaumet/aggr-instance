Today, we’re expanding Cognition into Japan, marking our first step into Asia.

This is an important milestone in our mission to build the future of software engineering with Devin, our autonomous AI software engineer, and to partner with teams pushing the boundaries of what great engineering looks like.

Building Cognition Japan

To lead this next chapter, we’re proud to welcome Takumi Masai as Japan President and General Manager.

Masai brings more than 30 years of experience in advanced system development and enterprise transformation in Japan. He has held senior leadership roles at IBM, Pivotal, Microsoft, Workday, and most recently served as President of Datadog Japan.

With Masai-san leading our efforts, we are building a dedicated local team to work directly with Japanese enterprises and partners, helping improve engineering productivity, unlock projects that were previously out of reach, and accelerate how software gets built and shipped.

We are already working with a growing set of leading organizations across Japan. DeNA, one of Japan’s leading technology companies, has used Devin to more than double operational efficiency across multiple engineering functions, including new service development, code quality improvement, and automation of routine workflows. Through our partnership with ULS Consulting, we have also supported Mizuho Securities in becoming one of the first large scale financial institutions in Japan to deploy Devin.

These examples reflect the broader momentum we are seeing across industries, from consumer platforms to highly regulated financial systems.
