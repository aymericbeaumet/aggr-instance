Today, Cognition crossed $1B in annualized revenue run rate.

We started Cognition in January 2024 because the world needs far more software than it can build. Every company is now a software company, from banks to automakers to governments, and every one of them has more to build than time to build it.

Less than two years after Devin became generally available, Devin works alongside engineering teams at GE Aerospace, Rivian, Rohlik, Exa, and many more. We asked a few of them to share what that looks like day to day.
