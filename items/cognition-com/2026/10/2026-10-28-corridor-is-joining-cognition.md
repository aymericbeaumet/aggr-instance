By Corridor Co-founders Jack Cable and Ashwin Ramaswami 2026.10.28

[Cognition x Corridor](https://player.vimeo.com/video/1240617696?h=03549b2b98&amp;badge=0&amp;autopause=0&amp;player_id=0&amp;app_id=58479&amp;title=0&amp;byline=0&amp;portrait=0)

Today, Corridor is joining Cognition, where we’ll be taking [Devin Security](https://devin.ai/security) to the next level.

We started Corridor almost two years ago with the mission of accelerating secure and autonomous software development. Since then, Corridor has become the security layer for AI-native teams, and we’ve seen security go from a niche concern to one of the most pressing issues of our time.

We’ve long admired the Cognition team. We’re especially excited to join forces with Russell Kaplan, Cognition’s President and one of our earliest advisors, and to work together with Alex Stamos, Cognition CSO, once again. Over the past few months, we’ve also gotten to know Cognition’s CTO Steven Hao and are looking forward to working closely with him.

For us, this mission is personal. We’ve spent our careers finding and fixing vulnerabilities in some of the world’s most critical systems and building secure software to be resilient against all kinds of attackers. AI agents can now execute end-to-end attack chains, and defenders need new tools to keep up. Companies will only stay secure by working now to address longstanding security debt.

The next 6–12 months represent the most consequential period in the history of cybersecurity. The threats are only increasing, and the time for companies to act and shore up their defenses is now. We couldn’t be more excited to join forces with Cognition and secure the world’s software.

We’re grateful to our team, our customers, our investors, and everyone who’s supported us along the way. What we’ve built at Corridor is paving the way for a more secure future, and we can’t wait to show you what we build next.

– Jack and Ashwin
