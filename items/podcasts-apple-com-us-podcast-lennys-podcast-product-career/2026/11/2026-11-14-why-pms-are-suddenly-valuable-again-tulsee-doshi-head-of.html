Lenny's Podcast: Product | Career | Growth

Tulsee Doshi is head of product for Google’s frontier AI models, including Gemini and other multimodal models, such as Nano Banana, Genie, Veo, and Lyria.

We discuss:

1. Why model development is not just engineering, but also like Snape’s potions class

2. Google’s full-stack frontier AI strategy

3. What PMs do inside a frontier AI lab

4. Tulsee’s 60-20-20 framework for balancing what works now and what’s almost working

5. How to write your first eval

6. The rising value of great PMs
