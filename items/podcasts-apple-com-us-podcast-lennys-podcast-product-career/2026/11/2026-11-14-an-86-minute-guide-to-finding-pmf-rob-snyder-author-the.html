Lenny's Podcast: Product | Career | Growth

Rob Snyder is the author of The Power of Pull and writes the newsletter The Physics of Startups. He has advised more than 50 startups on growing from zero to $1 million in ARR, drawing on his own experience as a founder who spent two years pushing a product the market didn’t want before accidentally stumbling onto what actually works. In this episode, we walk step by step through his framework for finding product-market fit, from coming up with an idea to closing your first handful of deals.

We discuss:

1. The PULL framework: Project, Unavoidable, List of options, Limitations

2. Where the best startup ideas come from

3. Tactics for cold outreach and running your first sales call

4. Why Rob advises against design partners

5. Goblin mode: what to do when nothing is working

6. How to apply the PULL framework inside larger companies
