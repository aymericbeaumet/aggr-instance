Lenny's Podcast: Product | Career | Growth

Muse is the #1 app in the App Store and arguably the fastest-growing AI consumer product of all time. **Mona Sarantakos** has led product on it since day one. She’s VP of Product at Meta Superintelligence Labs and a 10-year Meta veteran with over 20 years of experience as a PM. This is her first podcast interview.

**We discuss:**

1\. How a weekend with OpenClaw on a Raspberry Pi turned into Muse

2\. Why Muse waited seven months to launch, despite getting started before most of its competitors

3\. How Nat Friedman, Alex Wang, and Mark Zuckerberg each left their imprint on the product

4\. How the team uses “agent swarms” to surface user insights at scale

5\. The use case nobody on the team predicted

6\. How Jolly, a placeholder avatar, became an icon

7\. The two levers that actually move activation for an AI agent

8\. The #1 thing Mona looks for in PMs today
