**Chip Huyen** is a core developer on Nvidia’s Nemo platform, a former AI researcher at Netflix, and taught machine learning at Stanford. She’s a two-time founder and the author of two widely read books on AI, including *AI Engineering*, which has been the most-read book on the O’Reilly platform since its launch. Unlike many AI commentators, Chip has built multiple successful AI products and platforms and works directly with enterprises on their AI strategies, giving her unique visibility into what’s actually happening inside companies building AI products.

**We discuss:**

- What people think makes AI apps better vs. what actually makes AI apps better \[[04:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qbvY0dQgSJ4&t=268s)\]

- What pre-training vs. post-training is, and why fine-tuning should be your last resort \[[07:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qbvY0dQgSJ4&t=425s)\]

- How RLHF (reinforcement learning from human feedback) actually works \[[15:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qbvY0dQgSJ4&t=920s)\]

- Why data quality matters more than which vector database you choose \[[31:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qbvY0dQgSJ4&t=1915s)\]

- Why high performers are seeing the most gains from AI coding tools \[[45:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qbvY0dQgSJ4&t=2720s)\]

- Why most AI problems are actually UX issues \[[38:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qbvY0dQgSJ4&t=2330s)\]
