**Jason Droege** is the CEO of Scale AI, a company that provides foundational training data to every major AI lab. He previously co-founded Scour with Travis Kalanick and built Uber Eats from idea to $20 billion in revenue. In this conversation, Jason shares lessons from getting sued for $250 billion, discovering restaurant economics by weighing sandwich ingredients, and over 25 years of launching transformative technology businesses.

**What you’ll learn:**

1. What actually happened with Meta’s $14 billion investment in Scale AI \[[10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W99jdYZOlN0&t=627s)\]

2. Why AI models still need human experts to improve, and how that relationship is evolving \[[28:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W99jdYZOlN0&t=1698s)\]

3. How AI models learn from experts building websites and debugging code \[[18:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W99jdYZOlN0&t=1128s)\]

4. The business lessons from building Uber Eats from zero to $20 billion \[[41:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W99jdYZOlN0&t=2503s)\]

5. Why most enterprise data is useless for AI models today \[[25:53](https://youtu.be/W99jdYZOlN0?t=1553)\]

6. Why urgent daily problems beat super-valuable occasional problems when building products \[[46:03](https://youtu.be/W99jdYZOlN0?t=2763)\]

7. How to think independently when building new products and businesses \[[48:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W99jdYZOlN0&t=2899s)\]
