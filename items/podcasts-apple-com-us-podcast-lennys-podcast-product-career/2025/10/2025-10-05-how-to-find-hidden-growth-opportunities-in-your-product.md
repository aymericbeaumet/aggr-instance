**Albert Cheng** has led growth at three of the world’s most successful consumer subscription companies: Duolingo, Grammarly, and Chess.com. A former Google product manager (and serious pianist!), Albert developed a unique approach to finding and scaling growth opportunities through rapid experimentation and deep user psychology. His teams run 1,000 experiments a year, discovering counterintuitive insights that have driven tens of millions in revenue.

**What you’ll learn:**

1. How to use the explore-exploit framework to find new growth opportunities **\[[09:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2BKmNmnEj9w&t=577s)\]**

2. How showing premium features to free users doubled Grammarly’s upgrades to paid plans **\[[20:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2BKmNmnEj9w&t=1242s)\]**

3. What good retention looks like for a consumer subscription app **\[[28:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2BKmNmnEj9w&t=1683s)\]**

4. Why resurrected users drive 80% of mature product growth **\[[32:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2BKmNmnEj9w&t=1926s)\]**

5. Why “reverse trials” work better than time-based trials **\[[28:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2BKmNmnEj9w&t=1683s)\]**

6. The three pillars of successful gamification: core loop, metagame, and profile **\[[01:04:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2BKmNmnEj9w&t=3881s)\]**
