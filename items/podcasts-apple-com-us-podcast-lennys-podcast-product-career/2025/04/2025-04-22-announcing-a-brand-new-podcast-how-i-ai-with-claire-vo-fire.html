We’re living through one of the most extraordinary moments in history. How the entire world operates is changing with the rise of AI, and how we live and work is shifting along with it.

It’s both exciting and overwhelming.

Many of us are wondering if we’re falling behind, what we should be paying attention to (and ignoring), and how to best use the new tools and technologies.

That’s why I’m so unbelievably thrilled to announce the launch of a new podcast: “How I AI” with Claire Vo. Check it out now on YouTube / Spotify / Apple.

The mission of Claire’s new podcast is to show you how people from all walks of life have figured out how to use AI tools in their day-to-day life to improve the quality and efficiency of their work.

What makes this podcast unique is that it’s designed to give you highly practical and actionable tips, tricks, and workflows that you can copy and start using immediately. No philosophical debates about the future of humanity or pontification on what might be possible someday.

Each episode will be just about 30 minutes (often shorter), and the guest will live-demo one or two specific use cases they’ve found helpful, to show you how to do everything they describe.

I couldn’t imagine a more perfect host for this podcast than Claire Vo. Claire is an engineer, a three-time chief product officer, and a founder, and on the side has been building an AI product that is already making six figures. She also gave the #1 most popular talk at last year’s Lenny and Friends Summit.

What I love about Claire is that, unlike many people online, she doesn’t only talk about using AI—she lives and breathes it, and is constantly sharing what she’s learning. I can’t wait for you to learn from her and her amazing guests.

This is the first new podcast in the Lenny’s Podcast network. Depending on how it goes, we may add more down the road. And to be clear, nothing changes with Lenny’s Podcast—this is just more free content coming your way, every Monday.

If you’re building products, leading teams, starting a company, or just want to learn how to actually use AI in your life, this podcast is for you.
