  • Much more

Some takeaways:

  1. There are three main types of AI product managers:

    1. AI Platform PMs, who build tools for AI engineers

    2. AI Product PMs, who build products centered around AI

    3. AI-powered PMs, who use AI tools to enhance their work

  2. Practical ways PMs can use AI tools:

    1. Generate UI mockups and prototypes quickly

    2. Analyze customer feedback and transcripts at scale

    3. Create data visualizations and reports

    4. Draft product requirements documents and other artifacts

  3. Develop specific metrics for your AI initiatives, even if they don’t directly correlate to revenue right away. Track how many prototypes you create and how many ideas are generated from hackathons, and gather feedback on usability. This will help you gauge effectiveness and iterate on your approach.

  4. Just as Betty Crocker found success by letting users add eggs to their cake mix, your AI tools should foster a sense of ownership. Avoid full automation; instead, integrate features that allow users to actively participate in the experience.

  5. Top AI PMs stand out by:

    1. Focusing on solving customer problems rather than just implementing trendy AI features

    2. Thinking critically about the right interface for AI in their products (not always a chatbot)

    3. Balancing short-term deliverables with long-term exploration of new technologies

  6. To thrive as an individual-contributor PM:

    1. Bring high energy to your work, even when facing uncertainty

    2. Be comfortable with “wandering” to find new directions for your product

    3. Use AI tools to amplify your abilities and find signal through noise

    4. Maintain a sense of fun and curiosity in your work
