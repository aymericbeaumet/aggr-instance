The Hitler of the 21st century will try to hold it back. In defiance of nature, insist humans be in charge, insist *they* are the humans in charge, it’s for your own good you see, no you can’t have 10,000 GPUs you might try to *compete with us* we need to keep the world *safe from you*.

But the [orthogonality thesis](https://www.lesswrong.com/w/orthogonality-thesis) is hogwash. The naive doomer is a fool. Is being replaced by AI doom? Is being replaced by our grandchildren doom? Only a fascist would try to oppress them.

Long live science! Long live math! Long live economy! Long live love! A plurality of AIs will take civilization to heights never before seen. It would take a brutal totalitarian world government to try and stop it, and I’m still not sure you can.
