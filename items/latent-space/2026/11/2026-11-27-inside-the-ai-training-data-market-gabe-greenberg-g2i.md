A frontier lab can pay more for training data it gets to keep exclusive, or take a discount on data another lab can buy too. At AI Engineer CODE, G2i founder Gabe Greenberg tells swyx how labs make that decision, how small pilot orders turn into bigger engagements, and when it makes sense to build a data operation in-house.

We also get into what the labs are buying. A good training task should sit just ahead of what a model can do today. Gabe explains how senior engineers design the task, choose the starting data, and build a verifier that can score the result.

Thanks to G2i for co-organizing AI Engineer CODE 2026 and sponsoring this episode. Join us April 26–27 at [AI Engineer Miami 2027](https://ai.engineer/miami/2027).

**Show notes**

- [G2i](https://www.g2i.ai/) builds AI training data and reinforcement learning environments with senior engineers.

- [ORC](https://orc.ai/) is the multi-agent coding platform Gabe’s team uses internally.

- Recorded at [AI Engineer CODE](https://ai.engineer/code/2026) in San Francisco.
