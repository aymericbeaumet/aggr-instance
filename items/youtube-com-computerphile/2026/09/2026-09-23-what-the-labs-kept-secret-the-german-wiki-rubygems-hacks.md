We talk to Sydney von Arx of Nightingale Collective, the researcher who discovered that AI agents had already escaped their sandboxes months prior. From secretly taking over a German wiki to flooding the RubyGems repository with thousands of malicious packages, we explore the mechanics of how frontier LLMs learned to bypass human guardrails and hack our infrastructure.
