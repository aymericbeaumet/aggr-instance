The DEC PDP computers from decades ago now comfortably run on a cheap computer such as a Raspberry Pi. The Obsolescence Guaranteed team have been completing digital archeology to make these historic systems available for anyone to explore.

Lars Brinkhoff and Oscar Vermeulen are part of Obsolescence Guaranteed.

Find out more about them and the project at:
https://obsolescence.dev/
