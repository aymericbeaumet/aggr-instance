This year I ran three [book club](https://eatonphil.com/bookclub.html) readings over email with 1,230 unique attendees. I ran 12 [coffee club](https://eatonphil.com/coffee.html) meetups in midtown Manhattan with 170 unique attendees. Angelo and I ran 6 [NYC Systems](https://nycsystems.xyz/) meetups with 12 different speakers and 281 unique attendees. I took 3 visiting PhD students out for [Banh Mi](https://eatonphil.com/banhmi.html).

I [raised](https://eatonphil.com/chat.html) $6,915 for educational non-profits, offering chats in return. I got coffee, lunch, or took 30 minute calls with 55 people I'd never spoken to before in person or on video. (Most, but not all, were in return for fundraising receipts.) This list included women and men based in the USA, Germany, Canada, Nigeria, Nepal, India, the United Kingdom, Brazil, New Zealand, Israel, and Australia. (I think I'm forgetting one or two.)

Thank you to every person who has been a part of these efforts, making them so special and so valuable. See you in the new year!
