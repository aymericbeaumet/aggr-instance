Today, we're releasing **21 new flagship voices** for Grok Voice, joining the original five. All of them are multilingual and available now in the realtime [Voice Agent API](https://docs.x.ai/developers/model-capabilities/audio/speech-to-speech?campaign=new-voices-blog), the [Text to Speech API](https://docs.x.ai/developers/model-capabilities/audio/text-to-speech?campaign=new-voices-blog), and the new [**Grok Voice Agent Builder**](https://console.x.ai/team/default/voice/agents?campaign=new-voices-blog&utm_source=website&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=new-voices-blog). Meet the newcomers in the lineup:

Wellness

Support

Soft, empathetic, and soothing.

## [A voice for every use case](https://x.ai/news/new-flagship-voices#a-voice-for-every-use-case)

Each voice was cast for a specific job: support, characters, commentary, advertising, education. [Speech tags](https://docs.x.ai/developers/model-capabilities/audio/text-to-speech?campaign=new-voices-blog) like `[pause]` and `<whisper>` can be used to control the delivery. Hear five of them in their element:

Carina

Support

Thanks for your patience — I found the issue. \[pause\] Your account was still on the legacy plan, so I've moved you over and \<emphasis> applied the credit to this month. \</emphasis> \<soft> You're all set. \</soft> Anything else I can take care of?

Every voice is natively multilingual, supporting all of Grok Voice's 25+ languages.

Luna

闭上 眼睛， 静静 聆听 片刻。 每 一个 声音 都在 讲述 一个 故事， 每 一个 故事 都 值得 被 倾听。 无论 你 身在 世界 的 哪个 角落， 我 都很 高兴 你在 这里。

## [Improved naturalness for the original five](https://x.ai/news/new-flagship-voices#improved-naturalness-for-the-original-five)

Ara, Eve, Leo, Rex, and Sal got an upgrade alongside the new voices. They've been retrained with an improved recipe, so pacing, phrasing, and emphasis are more natural.

Warm and friendly.

To build a custom voice agent with one of the new voices, use the **Grok Voice Agent Builder** in the [xAI console](https://console.x.ai/team/default/voice/agents?campaign=new-voices-blog&utm_source=website&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=new-voices-blog). You can also [clone your own voice](https://docs.x.ai/developers/model-capabilities/audio/custom-voices?campaign=new-voices-blog) from about a minute of audio.
