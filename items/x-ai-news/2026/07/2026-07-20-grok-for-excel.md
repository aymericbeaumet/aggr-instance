**Today we're bringing Grok into Microsoft Excel.** Install the add-in to ask questions in plain English, write formulas from a description, or run scenarios in the workbook you already have open.

## [Ask questions about your data](https://x.ai/news/introducing-excel-addin#ask-questions-about-your-data)

Select a range and ask Grok what moved, why, and what stands out. Answers cite the cells they came from, and charts can drop directly into the sheet beside the data.

### [Write formulas and run scenarios](https://x.ai/news/introducing-excel-addin#write-formulas-and-run-scenarios)

Describe the outcome and Grok handles the syntax — averages, sorts, and fills land as normal edits in the formula bar. Test a downside case, rerun a forecast, or close out month-end without rebuilding the sheet by hand. The add-in can also leverage [connectors](https://grok.com/connectors); ask it to pull context from recent emails or files in SharePoint or Google Drive.

## [Get started](https://x.ai/news/introducing-excel-addin#get-started)

Grok for Excel is a free Microsoft 365 add-in. [Add it from the Microsoft Marketplace](https://marketplace.microsoft.com/en-us/product/office/WA200011056?tab=Overview), and work directly with Grok in your workbooks. Also available for [Word](https://x.ai/grok/word) and [PowerPoint](https://x.ai/grok/powerpoint).
