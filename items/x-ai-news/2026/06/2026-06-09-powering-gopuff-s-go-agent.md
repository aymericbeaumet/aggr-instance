Jun 9, 2026

Gopuff and SpaceXAI launched Go, an AI-powered shopping assistant built into the Gopuff app and powered by Grok text, audio, and image models.

* * *

The SpaceXAI team has collaborated closely with Gopuff to launch the Go agent - a personal shopping assistant that knows what you want and delivers in minutes - using frontier text, image, and audio models.

Go pairs Grok's frontier reasoning, voice, and image generation with Gopuff's 13 years of demand intelligence based on hundreds of millions of orders. Through SpaceXAI API tools, Go also leverages real-time signals from X and the web. The result is a shopping experience that anticipates what you need and delivers it in minutes from Gopuff's network of micro-fulfillment centers.

### [Building Go](https://x.ai/news/grok-gopuff#building-go)

Gopuff and SpaceXAI worked closely to build multimodal capabilities into the new Go agent. Go can build personalized carts for you before you open the app using past preferences and external signals like the weather. Over time, Go learns what you like and dislike, helping you order critical necessities when you need them.

Go also includes a new, visual shopping feed based on Grok Imagine. Using state-of-the-art image models, Go generates hyper-realistic scenes from Gopuff inventory, helping you visualize shopping carts and orders.

Go learns on every order to help you shop faster and more efficiently in the future. Go is available now in the Gopuff app on iOS and Android, starting in the US with the UK to follow.
