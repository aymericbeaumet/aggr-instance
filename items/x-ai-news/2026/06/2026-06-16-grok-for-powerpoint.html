Today we're bringing Grok into Microsoft PowerPoint. Using the add-in next to your deck, use Grok to research, write, and perfect your slides.

Go from prompts to PowerPoints

Share an outline with Grok to generate full slide decks, complete with research from web or X searches, diagrams, and even images. Or, work with Grok to add single slides, apply styles and themes, or restructure sections with a single instruction. Grok will come back to you to ask clarifying questions.

Research, writing, and styling

Grok can help you improve styling, tighten slide narratives, and even search for up-to-date information from the web. The add-in is also able to leverage your Grok connectors - ask it to write slides based on recent emails, or files in your SharePoint or Google Drive.

Get started

Grok for PowerPoint is a free Microsoft 365 add-in. Add it from the Microsoft Marketplace, and work directly with Grok in your presentations. Also available for Word and Excel.
