We're excited to share that Grok models are now natively available on Databricks Agent Bricks, Databricks' developer agent platform.

As part of the Databricks 2026 Data + AI Summit, Databricks is partnering with SpaceXAI to make Grok available alongside other frontier and open-source models in a single, governed platform.

Agent Bricks connects context (derived from data in Lakehouse) with control and choice, enabling engineering teams to effectively build AI agents that operate on large volumes of data.

Together with Grok on Amazon Bedrock, enterprises have more ways to run SpaceXAI models where their data already lives.

For more on Agent Bricks, see the Databricks announcement.
