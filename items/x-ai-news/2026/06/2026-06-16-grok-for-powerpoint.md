**Today we're bringing Grok into Microsoft PowerPoint.** Using the add-in next to your deck, use Grok to research, write, and perfect your slides.

## [Go from prompts to PowerPoints](https://x.ai/news/introducing-powerpoint-addin#go-from-prompts-to-powerpoints)

Share an outline with Grok to generate full slide decks, complete with research from web or X searches, diagrams, and even images. Or, work with Grok to add single slides, apply styles and themes, or restructure sections with a single instruction. Grok will come back to you to ask clarifying questions.

### [Research, writing, and styling](https://x.ai/news/introducing-powerpoint-addin#research-writing-and-styling)

Grok can help you improve styling, tighten slide narratives, and even search for up-to-date information from the web. The add-in is also able to leverage your [Grok connectors](https://grok.com/connectors) - ask it to write slides based on recent emails, or files in your SharePoint or Google Drive.

## [Get started](https://x.ai/news/introducing-powerpoint-addin#get-started)

Grok for PowerPoint is a free Microsoft 365 add-in. [Add it from the Microsoft Marketplace](https://marketplace.microsoft.com/en-us/product/office/WA200011057?tab=Overview), and work directly with Grok in your presentations. Also available for [Word](https://x.ai/grok/word) and [Excel](https://x.ai/grok/excel).
