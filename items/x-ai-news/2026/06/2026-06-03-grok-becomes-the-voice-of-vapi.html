What This Means for Vapi Developers

If you're already building on Vapi, your agents can now use Grok to power Vapi's text-to-speech. Select it from the dropdown and take it for a spin. You'll immediately get more natural, emotionally resonant conversations.

Additionally, Grok Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech are now available in the Vapi Dashboard.

New voices and advanced customization options (including custom voice cloning) are also available through the Grok Voice API for teams that want to integrate it directly in use cases like narration, podcasts, advertising, voiceover, and more.

Head over to your Vapi dashboard — the new default voices are live now. Or try Grok Voice directly via the API if you're building outside of Vapi.
