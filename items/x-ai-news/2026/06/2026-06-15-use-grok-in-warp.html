You can now use your Grok or X Premium subscription inside Warp, an agentic development environment built on the terminal and used by almost one million developers.

Your existing subscription provides access to Grok models, including grok-build-0.1, the model powering Grok Build.

Setup

  1. Download Warp from warp.dev/download and open it.
  2. Open the "Agent > Warp Agent" settings page and click "Connect SuperGrok subscription"
  3. Switch to the grok-build-0.1 model and start prompting.

That's it! More agents and integrations are coming soon to SuperGrok and X Premium subscribers. To subscribe, visit the pricing page.
