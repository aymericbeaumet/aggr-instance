A month’s worth of security breaches in a single day – will things get worse before they improve? Also: Anthropic accused of version control “rip off”, and more

*The Pulse is a series covering events, insights, and trends within Big Tech and startups.*

Today, we cover:

1. **Time to rethink software security because of AI**? A series of high-profile security incidents and breaches took place in a single day, many fueled by AI. Attackers gain advantage by using AI, and defenders will have to respond.

2. **Is Anthropic’s new version control system “a rip off**”? The creator of an open source, high-throughput version control server accuses Anthropic for ripping off their design, and presenting their product as novel by not giving credit to the pre-existing one. It’s not a good look for the leading AI lab.

3. **Industry Pulse**. Saudi Arabia made a “problematic” offer to pay alleged OpenAI spy’s bail, Shopify builds tooling for self-healing software, Foursquare’s sale disappoints, Claude Code gets a major speed boost with gzip, and more.

4. **Predictions for the future of software development**. Some [bold calls](https://thorstenball.com/blog/2026/09/19/what-i-believe-about-the-future-of-software-development/) by software engineer and Amp cofounder Thorsten Ball, such as that open source no longer makes sense.
