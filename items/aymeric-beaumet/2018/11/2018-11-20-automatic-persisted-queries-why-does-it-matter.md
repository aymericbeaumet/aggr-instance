[GraphQL](https://graphql.org/) is not to be presented anymore. One main drawback though in comparison to REST is the upstream bandwidth cost can become significant as the number of queries increases.

[Automatic Persisted Queries](https://www.apollographql.com/docs/apollo-server/performance/apq/) aims at addressing this problem by allowing to cache the queries server-side. They can then be queried again through a unique identifier sent by the backend.
