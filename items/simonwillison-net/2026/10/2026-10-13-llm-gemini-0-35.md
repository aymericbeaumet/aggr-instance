I added support to [llm-gemini](https://github.com/simonw/llm-gemini) for the [new Gemini 4 Argon](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/) model, made available today via the Gemini API and for customers of their $100/month subscription plan.

Here are [the low, medium and high pelicans](https://tools.simonwillison.net/markdown-svg-renderer?url=https%3A%2F%2Fgist.github.com%2Fsimonw%2Fd741e4420dea14c460f0108ac22dbc26). As expected, they are all excellent: Gemini has a track record of producing great SVGs, and are the only lab where I know their [staff have invested in SVG specifically](https://twitter.com/sunjiao123sun_/status/2024555516551372920) (and who have [boasted about their pelicans](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Feb/19/gemini-31-pro/#jeff-dean) before.)

Here's the "high" illustration, which cost me 17.166 cents:

![Stylish, accurate bicycle frame. Fish in the basket. Snazzy scarf. Feet on either side of the frame touching the pedals. Spokes, a little hat, a good beak and pouch, a somewhat fierce expression.](https://static.simonwillison.net/static/2026/gemini-4-argon-high.webp)

The pricing is interesting. At launch, Argon is $2/million input and $10/million output - the same price as GPT-6 Sol, half the price of Claude Opus 5.5 and 1/5th the price of Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra.

But on December 1st Argon is set to double in price - so it will be the same price as Opus 5.5.

Prices like this aren't really comparable these days since what really matters is how many tokens a model takes to achieve a goal within an agentic harness.

One useful clue for that comes from Artificial Analysis, who publish [the total cost to run their benchmark](https://artificialanalysis.ai/#total-cost-tabs) using each model. Argon is looking very good there - costing $2,407 to run the benchmark, putting it close to GLM-5.3 and significantly cheaper than OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta's latest models.

When the price doubles it will still be a little cheaper than GPT-6 Astra.
