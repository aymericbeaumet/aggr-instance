**[API credits benefit for eligible ChatGPT subscriptions](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001626-api-credits-benefit-for-eligible-chatgpt-subscriptions)**. Earlier this month Anthropic [announced a generous new benefit](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/7/claude-haiku-5-5/#api-credits) for their Max and Team subscriptions: $100, $200, and $500 per month subscribers would get the equivalent quantity of (expiring) credits to use with the Anthropic API.

OpenAI have now launched pretty much exactly the same deal: if you have a $100, $200, or $500 subscription plan with them you now get that same quantity of credits to apply to one of your API organizations.

I maintain subscriptions with both companies, so this is great news for me! I may even ship some public-facing demos now that the credits are already covered by my existing subscriptions.
