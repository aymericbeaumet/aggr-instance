**[Panache — RTS Belle Époque pour mobile](https://github.com/menufactory43/panache/)** ([via](https://twitter.com/Meffysto69/status/2112908755654365220 "@Meffysto69")) Well this is delightful: I [released Battle Drones](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/20/battledrones/) yesterday under an Apache 2.0 License and there's already a French language fork which reskins the game to take place in steampunk 1900s France and adds an animated intro!

> **Panache** est un jeu de stratégie en temps réel pour mobile et navigateur, dans la France de la Belle Époque (vers 1900) : dirigeables, Fardiers à vapeur, Creusot cuirassés, automates en képi, Gargantua la forteresse marchante — et beaucoup de panache.

You can [play Panache here](https://menufactory43.github.io/panache/) - it's worth clicking that link just to experience the new intro video.

So far it's mostly just aesthetic updates as opposed to gameplay changes. The music has had a French twist added to it as well.

The new steampunk models are so cool!

![The new buildings are gold and red, there are dirigibles and biplanes, and the Goliath stomping mech has had a total makeover.](https://static.simonwillison.net/static/2026/battledrones-french.jpg)

Here's a page with [the original Battle Drones models](https://simonw.github.io/battledrones/models.html) and that same page [with the new Panache alternatives](https://menufactory43.github.io/panache/models.html).

This is a comparison of the original and the new design for the Goliath:

![On the left: blue stomping mech robot, missile launcher shoulders, classic sci-fi robot look. On the right: gold, steampunk, much more 1900s French look.](https://static.simonwillison.net/static/2026/battledrones-goliath-diff.jpg)
