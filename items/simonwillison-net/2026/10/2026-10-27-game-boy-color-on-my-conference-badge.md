**[Game Boy Color on My Conference Badge](https://spf13.com/p/game-boy-color-on-my-conference-badge/)**. I'm at GitHub Universe this week and, [similar to last year](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Oct/28/github-universe-badge/), the conference badge is a full-blown color screen computer.

I've been hacking around with it again, and then I ran into with Steve Francia... who worked with Gemini to get a [full NES emulator](https://spf13.com/p/zelda-on-my-conference-badge/) working on it, and then went a step further and deployed an emulator for both the Game Boy and the Game Boy Color.

Which means, if you can obtain the ROM, you can run [The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Link%27s_Awakening) on your conference badge!

![Photo of the badge running Zelda, in full color, with visible touch buttons below the screen.](https://static.simonwillison.net/static/2026-10-27/IMG_9339.jpeg)

A neat feature of the badge is that you can turn it upside down and then use game controller touch buttons that are built into the badge body.
