**Release:** [llm-tools-datasette 0.1.1](https://github.com/simonw/llm-tools-datasette/releases/tag/0.1.1)

When I switched LLM over to [httpx2](https://github.com/pydantic/httpx2) in [LLM 0.33](https://llm.datasette.io/en/stable/changelog.html#v0-33) I forget that this would break any plugins which used `httpx` without directly depending on it. `llm-tools-datasette` was one of those plugins, now fixed.
