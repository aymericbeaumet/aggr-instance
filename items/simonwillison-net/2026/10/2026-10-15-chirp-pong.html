Tool Chirp Pong — Chirp Pong is a two-player paddle game where two phones pair and exchange the ball over sound, using each device's speaker and microphone instead of Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. The page offers a duel mode that pairs two phones through a chirp handshake and a practice mode against a CPU opponent, with selectable match lengths of 7, 11, or 15 points.
