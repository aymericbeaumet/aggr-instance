Gemini 4 Carbon only supports `low`, `medium` and `high` reasoning levels - but Gemini 4 Hydrogen and Gemini 4 Neon both have an extra `xhigh` level.

I missed that when I added those models to the `llm-gemini` plugin, which is now fixed in this dot release.

The `xhigh` setting for Hydrogen makes a *significant* difference. I updated my [Gemini 4 comparison grid](https://static.simonwillison.net/static/2026/gemini-4-carbon-neon-hydrogen-with-xhigh.html) - note how Hydrogen at high used 5,311 output tokens and cost 3.99 cents, but the same model at xhigh used 31,477 output tokens, cost 23.61 cents, and produced a pelican that's competitive with Gemini 4 Neon:
