[Yonkers Rises](https://tools.simonwillison.net/yonkers-rises) — Yonkers Rises is an animated 3D diorama of New York City in which a giant mech, cut from the Yonkers boundary, rises from the ground and walks through Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, and the Bronx while a stomp counter and mech height readout track its progress. The scene plays a stand-up sequence followed by a looping stomping tour, with buildings flattened under each footfall, and the status line changes as it moves across land or wades through water.

I realized I didn't know where Yonkers was, so I looked it up on Google Maps and then, to help embed its location and shape in my head (and to try out Claude Fable 5.2's 3D modeling and animation skills), I [prompted Fable 5.2 Medium](https://claude.ai/share/97443910-2e0a-4e17-9b9e-11d14d8ff65c) to:

> `Make a three js animation where yonkers stands up as a big mech (cool stand up motion) and átomos around manhattan and queens and Brooklyn, use openstreetmap mapping data`
