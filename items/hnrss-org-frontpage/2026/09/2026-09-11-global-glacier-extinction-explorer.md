How to explore

Navigate the map to discover glaciers worldwide. Select a warming scenario to see how projected extinction years shift under different futures. Hover over a glacier for a quick summary, or click it to explore its full profile — including area, elevation range, and projected extinction timing across scenarios. Use the **3D** button to tilt into a terrain view.
