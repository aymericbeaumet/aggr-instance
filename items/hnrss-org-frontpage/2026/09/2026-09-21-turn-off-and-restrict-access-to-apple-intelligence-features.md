You can turn off Apple Intelligence features like Siri AI (Beta),\* summarization features, and more. For more information on parental controls, see [Apple Devices for Kids and Families](https://support.apple.com/guide/child-safety/r1jy0rk9vfz8).

*Note:* Apple Intelligence on macOS 27 is not available in all languages or regions. Usage limits may apply.

To change these settings, do any of the following:

| Option                                                        | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Siri AI (Beta)                                                | Go to the [System Settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/aside/glos063f0019/27/mac/27) app ![](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/1cf9ed2f-f8ed-4854-bef2-1e31d23f1141) on your Mac, click Siri in the sidebar, then click Turn Off Siri. Click Turn Off Siri again to confirm.                                                                                              |
| Switch to Siri Classic                                        | To use Siri Classic instead of Siri Al, follow the steps above to turn off Siri. Click Turn On Siri, then select Use Siri Classic.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Messages summaries                                            | In the Messages app ![](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/4b491b8d-bb86-4d21-b34e-56f924240710) on your Mac, choose Messages > Settings, then turn off Summarize Messages.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Email summaries                                               | In the Mail app ![](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/e1cc491a-871b-46fb-b315-f61462a3938a) on your Mac, choose Mail > Settings, then click Viewing. Turn off Summarize Message Previews.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Notification summaries                                        | Go to the [System Settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/aside/glos063f0019/27/mac/27) app ![](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/1cf9ed2f-f8ed-4854-bef2-1e31d23f1141) on your Mac, click Notifications in the sidebar, then click Summarize Notifications. Turn off Summarize Notifications.                                                                               |
| Personalized Smart Replies in Mail                            | In the Mail app ![](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/e1cc491a-871b-46fb-b315-f61462a3938a) on your Mac, choose Mail > Settings, then click Composing. Turn off Personalize Smart Replies.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Voicemail suggestions in Phone                                | In the Phone app ![](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/b422bc76-6f63-478d-a813-b645dbdcf61a) on your Mac, choose Phone > Settings, then click Calls. Turn off Suggestions in Voicemail.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Call Context cards                                            | See [Customize apps Siri has access to](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/customize-siri-mchl31570551/27/mac/27#ba932cb2-ca86-4e88-b610-bf129e27622d).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Writing Prompts in Journal                                    | In the Journal app ![](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/7cb9c62a-3399-43d0-8e63-fd0f0618cf1a) your Mac, choose Journal > Settings > General, then turn off Get Writing Prompts.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Restrict access to Apple Intelligence features in Screen Time | See [Change Siri Restrictions settings in Screen Time](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/change-siri-restrictions-settings-screen-z25xrnykpuln/27/mac/27) for restricting access for yourself. For your child, see [Block or allow access and changes to features your kid’s device](https://support.apple.com/guide/child-safety/k4n88xwnliel) in Apple Devices for Kids and Families. |
| Change how you see suggestions from your apps                 | See [Customize apps Siri has access to](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/customize-siri-mchl31570551/27/mac/27#ba932cb2-ca86-4e88-b610-bf129e27622d).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
