I’ve stopped thinking.

My arms move slightly, the ball hits them, it arcs upwards towards the setter.

I used to think about it, how to put my arms together to form a platform, where to point my knuckles, whether my strength is coming from my feet or my arms.

But I’m tired now, I don’t remember how many balls I’ve hit and all I can do is hit the next one up.

I do my best work when I’m not thinking.

The french say “apprendre par cœur” (to learn by heart) the sufis say “teach me by taste, not by mind”, the programmers call it flow state, athletes call it muscle memory.

There is divinity in repetition, in knowing but not thinking. You cannot know by thinking, teach me by taste.

You don’t have to think so hard, it’s all inside you. Say yes quickly.
