With previously open source projects going closed, and artists increasingly concerned about posting their work online, is AI reversing the internet’s long-established trend toward openness?

In this sixth episode of Wading Through AI, [Demetri](https://demetrispanos.com/) and I consider the possibility that AI could substantially diminish, or even eventually end, the norms of the open internet.
