Recently, I gave a talk called *[The Root of the Root of All Evil](https://www.computerenhance.com/p/theroot)*. Like *[The Big OOPs](https://www.computerenhance.com/p/the-big-oops-anatomy-of-a-thirty)*, it was a research-intensive talk, so Demetri and I decided it might serve as a good spot test for how well the frontier models do on historical research.

In this episode of *Wading Through AI*, Demetri runs the major models on a query designed to reproduce the top-level research necessary for my talk. We evaluate how well they did, and speculate on the usefulness of current AIs for this kind of research.

*Demetri has provided a download of the research reports on his website. If you’d like to check them out yourself, you can [download them here](http://demetrispanos.com/casey_TROTROAE_research_AI.zip) .*
