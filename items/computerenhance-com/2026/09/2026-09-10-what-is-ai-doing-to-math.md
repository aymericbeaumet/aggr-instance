Over the course of 2026, AI has advanced from being a mathematician’s assistant to, in some cases, doing the majority of the work behind important new discoveries. What does this mean for formal mathematics as a discipline?

In this episode of *Wading Through AI*, Demetri walks Casey through three recent high-profile results made possible by the use of AI, and discusses the potential impact of this new level of automation on the field going forward.
