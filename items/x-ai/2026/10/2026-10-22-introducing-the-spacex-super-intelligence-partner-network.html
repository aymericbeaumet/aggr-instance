Today, we’re introducing the SpaceX Super Intelligence Partner Network, our official program for companies building products, solutions, and services with Grok for our joint prospects and customers.

The network brings together two types of partners:

  • Transformation Partners help customers deploy the Grok platform across the enterprise and create tangible business value.
  • Marketplace Partners build the plugins and product integrations that enable the Grok product suite to work with the tools customers already depend on.

Through the partner program, partners get access to the training, tools, and support they need in their engagements with our joint customers.

Building the Partner Network

More than 130 companies participated in the program’s early-access phase. Transformation Partners are building capacity through our Forward Deployed Engineer accreditation program, with engineers trained at more than 30 organizations. Marketplace Partners are seeing growing adoption of their products alongside Grok.

Across the network, partners are already helping customers in every industry and around the world, from startups to Fortune 20 companies.
