When software meets hardware, engineering principles remain largely the same, but the sensible defaults often change. **Kuldeep Singh** describes our experience modernizing a large IoT fleet of battery-powered sensors. Our familiar principles remained important; what changed was how we applied them. These practices emerged through experiments, failures, and operational experience—each challenge helping us replace a reasonable assumption with a more sensible default.
