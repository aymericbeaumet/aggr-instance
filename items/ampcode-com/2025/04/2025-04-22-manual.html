Amp is a coding agent and development environment built for the frontier.

  • Multi-Model: GPT-5.6, Claude Fable 5.1, fast models. Amp uses them all, for what each model is best at.
  • Orbs: Machines that Amp creates per thread, with your code, tools, and plugins already set up. Send a prompt, close your laptop, and the agent keeps working.
  • Everywhere: Start an orb from your iPhone, check on it from your Mac, and follow up in the TUI. The apps notify you when a thread is ready for your next message.
  • Opinionated: You’re always using the good parts of Amp. If we don’t use and love a feature, we kill it.
  • On the Frontier: Amp goes where the models take it. No backward compatibility, no legacy features.
  • Threads: You can save and share your interactions with Amp.

Want to go much deeper? Follow along on our Raising an Agent podcast and the Chronicle time capsules where we share what we’re learning as we build Amp.

Quickstart

  1. 1 Sign Up Create your account and sign in to Amp.
  2. 2 Sign in with ChatGPT Optional Use your ChatGPT Plus or Pro subscription for OpenAI models in Amp.
  3. 3 Add Your Code Create a project from a Git repository, or start without one.
  4. 4 Run an Agent in an Orb Give Amp a task in a fresh cloud environment with your project ready.
  5. 5 Get the macOS & iOS Apps Install Amp on your Mac, iPhone, and iPad to follow your agents anywhere.
  6. 6 Use Amp on the CLI Install Amp and work with it from your terminal.

Explore

Amp is the same agent and the same threads everywhere. Pick where you want to work:

  • Web. ampcode.com needs no install. Start a thread, and Amp works in an orb, a machine it creates for that thread.
  • Mac, iPhone, and iPad. The macOS & iOS apps add notifications, dictation, and sharing from other apps.
  • Terminal. The CLI runs Amp on your own machine, in your editor’s terminal, or from scripts.
