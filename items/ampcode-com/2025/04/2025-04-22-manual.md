Amp is a coding agent and development environment built for the frontier.

- **Multi-Model:** GPT-5.6, Claude Fable 5.1, fast models. Amp uses them all, for what each model is best at.
- **Orbs:** Machines that Amp creates per thread, with your code, tools, and plugins already set up. Send a prompt, close your laptop, and the agent keeps working.
- **Everywhere:** Start an orb from your iPhone, check on it from your Mac, and follow up in the TUI. The apps notify you when a thread is ready for your next message.
- **Opinionated:** You’re always using the good parts of Amp. If we don’t use and love a feature, we kill it.
- **On the Frontier:** Amp goes where the models take it. No backward compatibility, no legacy features.
- **Threads:** You can save and share your interactions with Amp.

*Want to go much deeper? Follow along on our [Raising an Agent podcast](https://ampcode.com/podcast) and the [Chronicle](https://ampcode.com/chronicle) time capsules where we share what we’re learning as we build Amp.*

## Quickstart

1. [1 Sign Up Create your account and sign in to Amp.](https://ampcode.com/auth/sign-up)
2. [2 Sign in with ChatGPT Optional Use your ChatGPT Plus or Pro subscription for OpenAI models in Amp.](https://ampcode.com/settings/model-routing?add=chatgpt)
3. [3 Add Your Code Create a project from a Git repository, or start without one.](https://ampcode.com/projects?newProject=1)
4. [4 Run an Agent in an Orb Give Amp a task in a fresh cloud environment with your project ready.](https://ampcode.com/docs/orbs/getting-started)
5. [5 Get the macOS & iOS Apps Install Amp on your Mac, iPhone, and iPad to follow your agents anywhere.](https://ampcode.com/docs/macos-and-ios)
6. [6 Use Amp on the CLI Install Amp and work with it from your terminal.](https://ampcode.com/docs/cli)

## Explore

Amp is the same agent and the same threads everywhere. Pick where you want to work:

- **Web.** [ampcode.com](https://ampcode.com/) needs no install. Start a thread, and Amp works in an [orb](https://ampcode.com/docs/orbs), a machine it creates for that thread.
- **Mac, iPhone, and iPad.** The [macOS & iOS apps](https://ampcode.com/docs/macos-and-ios) add notifications, dictation, and sharing from other apps.
- **Terminal.** The [CLI](https://ampcode.com/docs/cli) runs Amp on your own machine, in your editor’s terminal, or from scripts.

Then meet [Puck](https://ampcode.com/docs/puck), who launches and coordinates agents for you.
