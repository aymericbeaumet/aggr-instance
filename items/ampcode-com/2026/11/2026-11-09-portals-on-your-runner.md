[Portals](https://ampcode.com/docs/orbs/portals), which you know and love from using them in orbs, now work on your local machines too.

You can ask Amp to start a dev server or build a website on your machine and open it from the thread's **Portal** tab, regardless of where you are.

![A thread on a macOS runner asking Amp to create an overview page of all the file system use on the Mac and show it in a portal. Amp replies with a Disk Usage portal link, and the Portal pane beside it shows a Disk Usage web app: 241 GB of 383 GB used, a capacity bar split by category, and a donut chart showing that simulators and Xcode data take 155 GB.](https://ampcode.com/news/runner-portals-disk-usage-light-v2.avif)

Turn on **Allow Portals** under Settings › Runner in the [macOS app](https://ampcode.com/app), or start your runner with `amp --no-tui --remote-control-portals`.

Portals on your runner are only accessible to you. To share them with your workspace, start a [shared runner](https://ampcode.com/docs/cli/runners#share-a-runner-with-your-workspace) with `--share`, or turn on **Share With Workspace** in the macOS app, and everyone in the workspace can open its portals.

Read [Portals on Your Runner](https://ampcode.com/docs/cli/runners#portals-on-your-runner) for more details.
