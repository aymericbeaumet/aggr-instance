You can now talk to Puck in your car, so you can work more safely while driving without looking at a screen. In the rare case that you do need to look at something, it's displayed very big so a quick glance is enough.

The [Amp apps](https://ampcode.com/app) for iPhone and Android will automatically enter car mode if you use them there.

If you have a Tesla or any other device that can browse the web in your car, open [ampcode.com/car](https://ampcode.com/car).

(If you have kids in the car, they might enjoy throwing Puck in the air. Puck likes this.)

See more in the [Puck in the Car docs](https://ampcode.com/docs/puck#call-puck-from-your-car).
