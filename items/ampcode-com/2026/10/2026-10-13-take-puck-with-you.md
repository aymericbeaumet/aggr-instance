You can now talk to Puck from ChatGPT, Claude, and other agents via MCP.

![ChatGPT opens Puck MCP and shows an Orbs card with one running orb, then lists what Puck can do: find previous work, delegate coding, inspect repositories, track development, coordinate agents, automate workflows, manage Amp, and communicate through connected services.](https://static.ampcode.com/news/puck-mcp-in-chatgpt.avif)

After developing an idea with ChatGPT's voice mode on a walk, send it directly to Puck for implementation. Puck knows your projects in Amp and routes it to the right orb. In the same conversation, you can check in on your work in Amp directly from ChatGPT.

Puck MCP offers two tools: `puck` for interacting with your Puck in Amp, and `orbs` for showing an interactive view of your running orbs with links to their portals.

You're talking to the same Puck you use in Amp. It can look through your threads, find earlier work, and coordinate your agents.

To connect, open [Settings > Puck](https://ampcode.com/settings/puck). Click **Add to Claude**, or copy `https://ampcode.com/mcp` into ChatGPT or another MCP client, then sign in with your Amp account.

![The Puck in Other Apps card in Amp's Puck settings, with an Add to Claude button, a Copy URL button for ChatGPT, and the https://ampcode.com/mcp server URL for other MCP clients.](https://static.ampcode.com/news/puck-mcp-settings.avif)

See [Use Puck in Other Apps](https://ampcode.com/docs/puck#use-puck-in-other-apps) for details.
