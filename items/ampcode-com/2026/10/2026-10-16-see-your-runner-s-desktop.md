You can now see and control your macOS or Linux runner's desktop. This is nice for trying native apps that Amp builds for you, such as iOS apps:

Turn on **Share This Mac's Display** under Settings › Runner in the [macOS app](https://ampcode.com/app), or start your runner with `amp --no-tui --desktop`.

![The Runner tab in the Amp macOS app's settings, with Share This Mac's Display turned on and its Sharing status highlighted.](https://static.ampcode.com/news/runner-desktop-settings-light.png) ![The Runner tab in the Amp macOS app's settings, with Share This Mac's Display turned on and its Sharing status highlighted.](https://static.ampcode.com/news/runner-desktop-settings-dark.png)

Your Amp client connects directly to the runner, so it's nearly instant when the runner is on your local network or nearby.

Read [See and Control the Runner's Desktop](https://ampcode.com/docs/cli/runners#see-and-control-the-runners-desktop) for more details.
