Today, ChatGPT Enterprise is available through AWS Marketplace. The listing gives organizations a simpler way to discover, buy, and manage ChatGPT Enterprise using their existing AWS purchasing and billing relationship and to apply eligible spend toward their AWS commitments.

More than [1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-26-new-countries/). ChatGPT Enterprise brings that familiar experience to work with ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, Codex, and dots. With ChatGPT Enterprise now available in AWS Marketplace, AWS customers get the same models, features, and functionality available directly from OpenAI, with new features released at the same time.

These OpenAI products [complement the OpenAI models available in Amazon Bedrock](https://openai.com/index/openai-on-aws/). Developers can build AI applications and agents with OpenAI models in Amazon Bedrock, while teams across the business use ChatGPT Enterprise in their daily work. Organizations can choose the approach that fits their workloads and governance requirements.

Eligible customers can also access ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Financial Services through AWS Marketplace.

Contact your OpenAI or AWS account team to confirm eligibility and get started with ChatGPT Enterprise through AWS Marketplace [here](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-sscra4m7iracg).
