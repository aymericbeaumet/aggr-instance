Read the first AI response beamed back from our Project Suncatcher satellite. After launching TPUs into low-Earth orbit, our first Project Suncatcher prototype satellite returned the first-ever AI response generated by a data center-class Google TPU in space: a haiku composed onboard by Gemma 4. See how we broke open a 20-year bottleneck in quantum computing. In a landmark study published in Science, Google Quantum AI demonstrated that a technique called "magic state cultivation" works in practice on our Willow quantum processor, cutting errors roughly 40-fold compared to uncultivated states. Because producing high-purity magic states previously threatened to consume up to 95% of a quantum computer’s resources, this breakthrough clears a major hurdle on the path to large-scale, fault-tolerant quantum computers.
